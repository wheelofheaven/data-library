## Corpus-wide decisions (recommendations — Editors and Reviewers agree)

1. **bārāʾ = 'create'** in Isaiah, Amos 4:13, Deut 4:32. The Genesis-only 'shape' entry is NOT extended; flag it for a later review (its rationale cites a separate homonym and a wrong GKC locator).
2. **Prophets conventions follow Isaiah** for Isaiah and the minor prophets: 'God' for ʾĕlōhîm, ʾereṣ 'earth' (cosmic) / 'land' (territory), lower-case 'spirit', tôrâ 'teaching', maśśāʾ 'oracle', no quotation marks after 'Thus says YHWH', nəʾum YHWH 'declares YHWH'. Published Ezekiel ('Elohim', 'land', 'ruach') to be harmonised at its next pass.
3. **Names stay split by book family for now**: Torah 'Moses'/'Mitsrayim' (as published Exodus); Former Prophets transliterated with '-ite' gentilics (as Kings); Prophets and Writings English forms. One corpus-wide naming decision later.
4. **ḥērem = 'devote(d) to destruction'** (Joshua; Num 21:2–3 aligned); promote to a central entry.
5. **tərûmâ = 'oblation'** (with hērîm 'set apart') corpus-wide.
6. **tôrâ**: 'law' in the Torah and Kings, 'teaching' in the Prophets.
7. **ḥesed = 'kindness'**; **bĕnê hāʾādām = 'the sons of the human'** (as Genesis 11:5).
8. **1 Sam 6:18 'the great stone'** — the only departure from the MT (masculine ʾāḇēl cannot agree); a narrow exception to the MT-baseline rule.
9. **Ps 8:6 'a little less than Elohim'** (no article, as the Hebrew); **Zech 4:14 'the two sons of fresh oil'** (inferred).
