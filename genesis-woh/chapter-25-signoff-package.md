## The chapter's character

A pivot chapter. Abraham's death + funeral closes the Abraham cycle; the Ishmael toledot finalizes that line; Isaac's toledot opens; Rebekah's barrenness + the divine oracle frames the younger-displaces-elder pattern that drives the Jacob cycle; the birthright sale at the lentil-stew is the chapter's structural turn. Multiple reception-sensitive moments: the oracle (Rom 9:12), the despising of the birthright (Heb 12:16, Genesis Rabbah 63:11), the ANE Nuzi parallels for the birthright-sale.
