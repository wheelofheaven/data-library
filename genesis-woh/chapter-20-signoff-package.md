## The chapter's three foundational firsts

### `navi-first-occurrence-abraham` (v 7) — MANDATORY MAJOR

The **first occurrence of *navi* (prophet) in the entire Hebrew Bible.** Abraham is designated *navi* in a function-defining context — the prophet prays for Abimelech and Abimelech lives. The Reviewer verified:
> *"Gen 20:7 is indeed the first occurrence in the HB; no prior in Gen 1-19. Function-definition as intercessor is textually carried by the parataxis ki-navi hu v-yitpallel. Pre-Mosaic placement defensible per Sarna JPS pp. 142-143; Lindblom 1962; Wilson 1980; Petersen 2002."*

### `hithpallel-first-prayer-occurrence` (v 17) — MANDATORY MAJOR

The **first occurrence of *hithpallel* (to pray) in the entire Hebrew Bible.** Structural pair with v 7 — the *navi* introduced at v 7 prays at v 17. Reviewer verified:
> *"Cross-corpus distribution (Num 11/21, Deut 9, 1 Sam 1/7/12, 1 Kgs 8 eleven-occurrence pericope, Jer 7/11/14 prohibition-formula) accurately documented. Greenberg 1983 'self-assessment' minority etymology appropriately preserved alongside HALOT/BDB mainstream 'intercede.'"*

### `divine-dream-communication-formula` (v 3)

First instance of Elohim-in-a-dream to a non-Israelite ruler. Cross-corpus forward-loaded: Gen 28:12 (Jacob's ladder), 31:24 (Laban), 37:5-11 (Joseph), 41:1-32 (Pharaoh), 1 Kgs 3:5 (Solomon at Gibeon), Num 12:6 (Mosaic dream-prophecy formula).
