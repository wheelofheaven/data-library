4. **v 6 — `yefeh-to'ar vifeh mar'eh` (Rachel cross-reference).** Resolved by **overlay entry + commentary** (`yefeh-toar-beauty-formula-rachel-yosef`). 6. **v 20 — `beit ha-sohar` (prison / round-house).** Resolved by **central entry + commentary** (`beit-ha-sohar-prison-round-house`). The *sohar* etymology (round-house / Egyptian loanword / by-form) is documented without adjudication; ASV-baseline 'prison' / 'keeper of the prison' retained; distinguished from the Gen 37 *bor* and the Gen 40:15/41:14 *beit ha-bor*. **Central** by cross-cycle recurrence into Gen 40:3 + 5 (the dream-interpretation setting) and the structural role of the prison in Gen 40–41.
