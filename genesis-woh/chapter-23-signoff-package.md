## The chapter's character

A low-lens-leverage legal-narrative chapter — the first detailed land-transaction in the Hebrew Bible, the first patriarchal burial-place, and the introduction of the Machpelah-Hebron tomb-site that will receive Abraham (Gen 25:9), Isaac (49:31), Rebekah (49:31), Leah (49:31), and Jacob (50:13).

The two distinctive WoH translation choices both preserve the Hebrew lexical-form:
- **"sons of Het"** + gentilic **"the Hittite"** — preserves MT lexical-form distinction
- **"prince of Elohim"** at v 6 — literal, preserving MT *Elohim* per project policy; departs from modern critical default ("mighty prince" in NJPS/NRSV/Speiser which reads *Elohim* as superlative)

Reviewer-verified: *"The two distinctive WoH choices are lexical-MT preservation, fully apparatus-supported, and would not strike a credentialed scholar as interpretation-disguised-as-translation."*
