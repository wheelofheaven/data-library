## Ratified posture: doublet sibling-overlay pattern

**Posture ratified at sign-off (2026-05-26):** when a load-bearing event (renaming, theophany, etymology) appears twice in different documentary strata, the project creates **sibling overlay entries** — one per attestation, each preserving its source-stratum's distinctive framing, with explicit cross-references between them. This becomes the project default for future doublet encounters.

Applied at Gen 35:
- `yisrael-name-aetiology-sarah-im-elohim` (Gen 32:29 — Penuel etymology; overlay v1.0)
- `yisrael-second-renaming-beit-el-p-doublet` (Gen 35:10 — P-doublet documentary observation; overlay v1.3, NEW)

## Project milestone: twelve tribes complete

The twelve-tribes name-aetiology arc reaches completion at Gen 35:18 (Binyamin = twelfth):
- **Gen 29:32-35** (Le'ah's firstborn quartet): Re'uven, Shim'on, Levi, Yehudah → `four-leah-firstborn-name-etymologies-cluster` (central)
- **Gen 30:6-13** (Bilhah + Zilpah substitute-wife sons): Dan, Naftali, Gad, Asher → `bilhah-zilpah-handmaid-sons-aetiology-cluster` (central)
- **Gen 30:18-21** (Le'ah's second cluster): Yissakhar, Zevulun, Dinah → `leah-second-sons-aetiology-cluster` (central)
- **Gen 30:23-24** (Rachel's first): Yosef → `yosef-double-name-aetiology` (central)
- **Gen 35:18** (Rachel's second + last): Binyamin → `binyamin-ben-oni-twelfth-tribe-aetiology` (overlay)

Dinah is included in Gen 30 but is not a tribe; the count remains 12 sons.
