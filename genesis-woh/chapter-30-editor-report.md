## Speculative entries requiring sign-off

**None.** No `claim_type: speculative` entries were added for this chapter.

The four candidates the brief flagged for Reviewer attention
(`jacob-rod-trick-sympathetic-fertility`, `nichesh-laban-divination`,
`yosef-double-name-aetiology`, `duda'im-mandrakes`) were all
shippable as `direct` on the following grammatical-and-philological
grounds:

- **`jacob-rod-trick-sympathetic-fertility`** — the sympathetic-magic
  reading is the **mainstream modern-critical philological consensus**
  (Westermann, Speiser, Sarna, Alter, Hamilton, Wenham), supported by
  the Mesopotamian *šumma izbu* omen-corpus (Leichty 1970 canonical
  edition) and the long-history-of-the-belief survey (Albright 1957;
  Aristotle *Hist. Anim.* 7.6; Pliny *Nat. Hist.* 7.51). The
  technique-versus-providence doublet (Gen 30:37-42 vs. Gen 31:9-13)
  is verifiable by reading the two passages in sequence — nothing
  about the entry exceeds documentary-grammatical evidence. The
  WoH-lens-relevant material (the rod-trick as an ANE folk-veterinary
  procedure rather than as miraculous-providence) is what the
  philological consensus already says.
- **`nichesh-laban-divination`** — the technical-cultic preservation
  *I have divined* (Speiser/Westermann/NRSV-tradition) over the
  ASV-smoothing *I have learned by experience* is a **standard
  philological choice** documented across modern critical commentaries.
  The Mosaic-prohibition cross-corpus trail (Lev 19:26, Deut 18:10,
  2 Kgs 17:17, 21:6, 2 Chr 33:6) is grammatically explicit. Lavan's
  later teraphim-ownership (Gen 31:19, 34-35) characterizes him as a
  divination-practitioner by HB-internal evidence.
- **`yosef-double-name-aetiology`** — the doublet structure is
  grammatically explicit in successive verses (vv 23, 24); the
  documentary-source-attribution (E/J) is the classical critical
  reading (Wellhausen; Friedman 2003); the final-form-canonical
  reading (Westermann; Sarna; Alter) treats the doublet as
  deliberate-compositional regardless of source-history. Both
  readings converge on the same narrative-effect.
- **`duda'im-mandrakes`** — the botanical-identification with
  *Mandragora officinarum / autumnalis* is the **modern-botanical
  consensus** (Zohary 1982; Moldenke-Moldenke 1952). The ANE
  comparative materia-medica (Egyptian *rrmt*, Akkadian *pıllu*,
  Greek Theophrastus / Dioscorides / Pliny) is documented cuneiform,
  papyrological, and classical evidence across multiple cultural
  corpora. The *dod / duda'im* etymological-link is grammatically
  transparent. The narrator's non-credit of the mandrakes for the
  v 17 conception is verifiable by reading.

All four would be candidates for speculative-treatment **only** if the
WoH-track wished to assert a stronger claim than the entry currently
makes — e.g., reading the rod-trick as genetic-knowledge-encoded
elsewhere in the Elohim-corpus, or reading Lavan's divination as a
positive-attestation of pre-Mosaic mantic-practice as canonical. The
current entries make no such claims; they document the philological
consensus and let the reader draw conclusions.

**Reviewer attention is nevertheless requested** for these four
entries as **load-bearing chapter-anchors** — the chapter cannot be
read without them and they shape downstream-narrative understanding
(esp. the rod-trick / Gen 31:9-13 reframe that will recur in
chapter 31's editor-pass).

## Translation choices worth flagging

1. **v 27 — *nichashti*.** The Translator's draft already adopted
   Alter/Speiser's *I have divined* over the ASV smoothing *I have
   learned by experience*. Editor concurred. This is a substantive
   philological choice that other translation projects (NRSV, NJPS)
   are inconsistent on. The choice supports a coherent
   Lavan-as-divination-practitioner characterization through the
   chapter-cycle.

2. **v 8 — *naftulei Elohim*.** Editor preserved the ASV-baseline
   *mighty wrestlings* (superlative-Elohim reading) in the running
   English while flagging the genitive-divine alternative (Alter,
   Speiser) in commentary. The choice is conservative; a Reviewer
   wishing to lean toward the genitive-divine reading would have
   internal support but would diverge from the consensus.

3. **v 20 — Zevulun's doubled-pun.** Editor preserved both puns
   explicitly (*endowed me with a good dowry; now will my husband
   dwell with me*). This is the structurally-correct choice; the
   single-pun alternatives (modern smoothings) erase Speiser's
   redactor-doublet observation.

4. **v 37 — botanical-identifications.** Editor preserved the
   ASV-Vulgate-consensus *poplar / almond / plane-tree* in the
   running English. The Rashi/Ibn Ezra *storax* alternative for
   *livneh* is documented in the glossary apparatus but not
   adopted in the running English. The plane-tree identification
   (rather than *chestnut*, KJV) is the modern-botanical consensus.
