## Founder rulings (2026-11-28: all accepted as recommended)

1. **YHWH / YHWH-Elohim.** Recommended **A**: keep "the creators" for both. This is what the canon writes every time it explains a Genesis YHWH verse, and it keeps the Hebrew name shift visible: Gen 1 has "the Sky People", Gen 2–11 has "the creators". (B: "Yahweh" for the individual Council president. The canon never credits him with a Genesis act; at 3:14 it names the government. C: "the Sky People" everywhere.)
2. **"I/me" in direct speech after a plural subject** (6:13, 9:9 …). Recommended: keep the singular as printed. One speaks for the group, and the Hebrew is singular.
3. **Creation "day".** Recommended: "age" ("age one", "the sixth age"), with the seventh day included as an inferred extension.
4. **Tree of knowledge.** Recommended: "the scientific books" for the named tree only (2:9, 2:17). "Eat" stays, because the canon itself calls the eating an image. (Alternative: "the tree of scientific knowledge".)
5. **Garden.** Recommended: decode only 3:23–24 as "the creators' residence". Elsewhere "garden" already fits the canon's "earthly paradise" territory.
6. **Ark → rocket** leaves "gopher wood", "pitch" and 7:18 "went on the face of the waters", which TBWTT 2:60 contradicts. Recommended: keep the shape and let the Meaning note correct it.
7. **Pairs → cells.** Recommended: apply only to the instruction (6:19–20). The boarding narrative stays literal.
8. **"Evil" → "the desire to progress".** Recommended: swap only *ra* ("evil"). "Wickedness" (*ra'at*) stays.
9. **Offerings.** Recommended: "food" in 4:3–5 only. 8:20's burnt offerings stay.
10. **Edge verses.** Recommended: in 4:1 keep "YHWH himself!", because "the creators themselves!" misleads; in 4:26 use "the name of the creators"; in 9:26 use "the creators, the Sky People of Shem".
11. **Tanninim.** Recommended: keep "the great dragons", which is the canon's own word.
12. **Meaning-lens naming of the YHWH strand.** Recommended: "the creators", with "those who came from the sky" at the first Elohim mention in each chapter.
13. **Serpent wording.** Recommended: accept "the small group of creators", including 3:14's "The creators said to the small group of creators", which is the canon's own contrast.

Agreement rule: after a swap, pronouns and verbs in the same sentence follow the new plural subject ("he ceased" → "they ceased", "the Elohim knows" → "the Sky People know"). Antecedents need a human pass.
