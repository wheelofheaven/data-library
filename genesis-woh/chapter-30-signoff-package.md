## Reviewer's verbatim verifications

**On v 27 (the chapter's most substantive translation shift):**
> *"The Speiser/Alter rendering 'I have divined' over ASV's smoothing 'I have learned by experience' is fully defensible. BDB s.v. nachash II p. 638 gives 'practise divination' as the primary Piel sense; HALOT concurs; Sarna JPS p. 214 explicitly defends the technical-cultic force; the cross-corpus prohibition-trail and the Balaam parallel are grammatically explicit. Laban's downstream teraphim-ownership (Gen 31:19, 34-35) supports the characterization arc."*

**On the rod-trick (vv 37-43):**
> *"Sympathetic-magic / maternal-impression reading is the mainstream modern critical consensus. Mesopotamian *šumma izbu* parallels documented in Leichty's canonical TCS 4, 1970 edition. Critically: the lens-relevant material lives in the commentary, not in the English running text — the verses render as plain pastoral ASV-baseline."*

**On the Joseph doublet (vv 23-24):**
> *"*Asaf* (qal perfect, Elohim, backward-looking) + *yasaf* (hiphil-jussive precative, YHWH, forward-looking) in successive verses is unique in the patriarchal name-aetiology corpus. Both documentary-critical and final-form readings converge on the same narrative-effect."*
