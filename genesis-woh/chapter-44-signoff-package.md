# Genesis 44 — sign-off package

**Status:** reviewer-approved (clean approve sweep; sign-off is formality)
**Chapter:** 44 — **The moral climax of the Yosef cycle.** The silver cup planted in Binyamin's sack; Yosef's steward overtakes the brothers ("Wherefore have ye rewarded evil for good?"); the cup found in Binyamin's after the search from eldest to youngest; the brothers rend their garments; they fall before Yosef (*va-yippelu lefanav artzah* — the third *hishtachavah*-fulfilment). Yehudah's first response: *ha-Elohim matza et-avon avadekha* "Elohim hath found out the iniquity of thy servants." Yosef rejects corporate substitution. **Yehudah's great speech** (the longest in Genesis) — recounting the family history, Ya'aqov's love for Binyamin, the *seivah*-to-She'ol grief that Binyamin's loss would cause, the *arav*-surety he pledged (callback to Gen 43:9), and the **substitution-offer**: *yeshev-na avdekha tachat ha-na'ar* "let thy servant abide *instead of* the lad." The chapter ends mid-speech — Yehudah, who proposed the Gen 37:26-27 sale, offers himself as substitute for Rachel's other son.
