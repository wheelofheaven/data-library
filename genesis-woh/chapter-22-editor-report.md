## Items the Reviewer should attend to

### `nisah-divine-testing` — theological-problem framing

**Source:** נִסָּה (piel of n-s-h)
**WoH choice:** *tested* (NJPS/NRSV; modern critical default)
**Issue:** The verse generates the classical theological-problem of
divine testing (omniscient-deity-testing crux). The entry presents
four scholarly positions in parallel — Maimonidean pedagogical,
patristic disclosure, open-theism real-epistemic, modern critical
narrative-anthropomorphism — and preempts none. Kierkegaard, Auerbach,
Derrida noted as downstream philosophical-reception, not preempted.
**Recommendation:** ship. The four-position presentation is the
correct editorial discipline for the chapter's most-discussed
theological crux.

### `eretz-ha-moriah-temple-mount-identification` — claim_type call

**Source:** אֶרֶץ הַמֹּרִיָּה
**WoH choice:** *the land of Moriah* (MT preserved)
**Issue:** Editor's call between `direct` and `inferred`. The
place-name itself is grammatically explicit (direct), but the
Temple-Mount identification rests on the Chronicler's 2 Chr 3:1
datum — a downstream reading rather than an explicit Gen 22
statement. Modern critical scholarship is divided between accepting
the Chronicler's identification (Sarna, conservative-evangelical
tradition) and reading it as retrojection (Westermann, Speiser).
**Decision taken:** `claim_type=inferred` — the *inferred* signals
that the identification is a defensible scholarly reading consistent
with the canonical Hebrew Bible's own cross-reference but not a
grammatically explicit feature of Gen 22 itself.
**Recommendation:** Reviewer to confirm the `inferred` call. The
alternative (`direct`) would also be defensible if the Reviewer
prefers to emphasize the canonical-cross-reference status as
direct-evidence.

### `aqad-bind-aqedah-hapax` — verify hapax status

**Source:** וַיַּעֲקֹד (qal of *aqad*)
**WoH choice:** *bound*
**Issue:** Entry claims qal verbal *aqad* is a **Pentateuchal
hapax**. The morphologically-related noun *aqudim* (*striped,
banded*) recurs at Gen 30:35, 39, 40 and 31:8, 10, 12 — but in the
distinctly-different *striped-flock* sense, not the verbal
*to bind* sense. The qal verbal sense *to bind* is unique to Gen
22:9 in the entire Pentateuch.
**Recommendation:** ship. The hapax status is verifiable by lexical
search; the rabbinic-tradition reception (Mishnah Tamid 4:1; Genesis
Rabbah 56; b. Rosh Hashanah 16a, 26a; Spiegel 1967; Levenson 1993
ch. 8) confirms the lexeme's foundational status for the entire
*aqedah*-reception-tradition.

### `ata-yadati-divine-learning-question` — multiple-readings parallel

**Source:** עַתָּה יָדַעְתִּי
**WoH choice:** *now I know* (ASV-baseline literal)
**Issue:** The phrase generates the omniscience-and-test
theological-crux. Entry presents four parallel positions
(Maimonidean, patristic-disclosure, open-theism, narrative-
anthropomorphism-modern-critical-default). The editorial discipline
is to present all four without preempting any; the translation
preserves the literal phrase and lets the apparatus carry the
theological-crux.
**Recommendation:** ship. The four-position presentation matches
the *nisah-divine-testing* entry's parallel discipline and is the
correct editorial register for the chapter's most-debated theological
phrase.

### `ayil-ram-substitution-achar-echad-variant` — substitutionary-sacrifice + textual variant

**Source:** אַיִל אַחַר
**WoH choice:** *a ram behind him* (MT *achar* preserved)
**Issue:** Two distinct cruxes: (a) the substitutionary-sacrifice
theology (rabbinic *zecher aqedat Yitzchaq*; Christian vicarious-
atonement reception from Hebrews 11:17-19; Islamic Sura 37:107
*dhibhin azim*) — all flagged-not-preempted; (b) the textual variant
MT *achar* (*behind*) vs. LXX/Peshitta/Vulgate *echad* (*one*)
resting on *resh/dalet* (ר/ד) consonantal confusion. Modern critical
positions: Sarna/Wenham/Hamilton preserve MT as lectio difficilior;
Speiser and Westermann (cautious) prefer LXX *echad*; Skinner and
Driver treat as unresolvable. WoH translation preserves MT per
established textual-conservative practice (cf. `al-roi-and-halom-
elohim-variant`).
**Recommendation:** ship. The MT-preservation is consistent with
WoH policy at parallel cruxes; the variant is documented in the
entry and in the per-verse commentary.

### `bi-nishbati-divine-self-oath` — first ne'um YHWH + Hebrews 6 reception

**Source:** בִּי נִשְׁבַּעְתִּי נְאֻם־יְהוָה
**WoH choice:** *By myself I have sworn, says YHWH*
**Issue:** Verse contains two editorially-marked first-canonical-
occurrence formulae: (a) the divine-self-oath *bi nishbati* — the
foundational instance of the divine-self-oath formula across the
entire HB, with cross-corpus distribution to Isa 45:23 (Rom 14:11 /
Phil 2:10-11 reception), Jer 22:5, 49:13, 51:14, Amos 6:8; (b) the
prophetic-utterance formula *n'um YHWH* — the chronological-canonical
first occurrence in the Hebrew Bible (~365 cross-corpus occurrences
overwhelmingly in the prophetic corpus). **Hebrews 6:13-14** explicitly
cites Gen 22:16-17 as the locus of the divine-self-oath; reception
flagged-not-preempted.
**Recommendation:** ship. The two first-canonical-occurrence statuses
are verifiable; the Hebrews-reception is the most-cited NT-engagement
with the verse and is correctly held as downstream.

## Reception-sensitivity items (all flagged-not-preempted)

The chapter is the densest-reception-history pericope of the Abraham
cycle. The Editor has held each reception-tradition strictly as
downstream-not-preempted at every relevant entry. The Reviewer
should verify the discipline at each point:

- **Kierkegaardian existential reading** (*Fear and Trembling*
  1843; the *teleological suspension of the ethical*) — flagged
  downstream at `nisah-divine-testing`. The wider continental-
  philosophical reception (Levinas, Derrida *The Gift of Death*
  1995, Caputo) flagged at `ata-yadati-divine-learning-question`.
- **Christian Hebrews 6 + Hebrews 11 + sacrificial-typology
  reception** — flagged at `bi-nishbati-divine-self-oath` (Heb
  6:13-14), at `ayil-ram-substitution-achar-echad-variant` (Heb
  11:17-19; the patristic and Reformation atonement-theology),
  at v 5 commentary (Heb 11:19 resurrection-typology of *we will
  return*), at `ba-yom-ha-shlishi-third-day-formula` (1 Cor 15:4;
  Luke 24:7, 21, 46; Matt 12:40 Jonah-typology), and at `yechid-
  only-one-formula` (Vulgate *unigenitum*; John 3:16 *monogenes*;
  Heb 11:17-19).
- **Islamic Quran 37:101-113 reception** — flagged at `yechid-only-
  one-formula` (early tafsir tradition divided on Yitzchaq vs.
  Yishma'el; dominant Sunni Yishma'el-identification per Firestone
  *Journeys in Holy Lands* 1990), at `eretz-ha-moriah-temple-mount-
  identification` (al-Tabari and Ibn Kathir on Mina-vs-Levant; the
  Dome of the Rock identification), at `ayil-ram-substitution-
  achar-echad-variant` (Sura 37:107 *dhibhin azim*), at `ata-
  yadati-divine-learning-question` (the Quranic *vision* / *ru'ya*
  motif).
- **Jewish Akedah liturgical reception** — flagged at `aqad-bind-
  aqedah-hapax` (Mishnah Tamid 4:1; the Rosh Hashanah *Akedah*
  liturgy; *piyyutim*; the *zechut aqedat Yitzchaq* tradition;
  Spiegel 1967; Levenson 1993 chs 7-10; Vermes 1973), at `ayil-
  ram-substitution-achar-echad-variant` (the *shofar*-and-ram's-
  horns connection), at `ba-yom-ha-shlishi-third-day-formula`
  (the third-day *zechut avot* tradition).

At no point is project-distinctive WoH synthesis preempted in the
glossary entries or per-verse commentary; all WoH-specific readings
of the *aqedah* are reserved for the wiki and long-form material
downstream.
