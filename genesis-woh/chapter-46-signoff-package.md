# Genesis 46 — sign-off package

**Status:** reviewer-approved (clean approve sweep; sign-off is formality)
**Chapter:** 46 — **The descent to Mitsrayim.** Yisra'el journeys with all he has to Be'er-Sheva, offers sacrifices to *Elohei aviv Yitzchaq* (the last patriarchal sacrifice before leaving the land). The doubled-name-call theophany: *Ya'aqov, Ya'aqov... hineni*. **The descent-and-return covenant-promise:** *anokhi ha-El Elohei avikha al-tira me-redah Mitzraymah ki-le-goy gadol asimkha sham; anokhi ered immekha Mitzraymah ve-anokhi a'alkha gam-aloh* "I am the El, the Elohim of thy father; fear not to go down to Mitsrayim... I will go down with thee, and I will also surely bring thee up again" — the seed of the Exodus. **The seventy-souls genealogy** (vv8-27 — one of Genesis's three foundational archival-document insertions, with Gen 5 and Gen 10): Le'ah's 33, Zilpah's 16, Rachel's 14, Bilhah's 7 = *shiv'im nefesh* (LXX reads 75; Acts 7:14 echo). The Ya'aqov-Yosef reunion at Goshen — *va-yippol al-tzavarav va-yevk... amutah ha-pa'am acharei re'oti et-panekha ki odkha chai* "Now let me die, since I have seen thy face, because thou art yet alive." Yosef's instructions for the Pharaoh-audience (the shepherds-as-*to'evah* observation).
