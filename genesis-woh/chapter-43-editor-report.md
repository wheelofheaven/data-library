2. **v 8 — *ha-na'ar* of Binyamin (extension of existing overlay).** Resolved by **overlay `appliesTo` extension** of `naar-lad-youth-or-attendant` to include GEN-WOH-43:8. The entry's rationale documents that the lexeme covers the wide age-range and is not strictly age-determined; the Gen 43:8 application reads as the affective 'young-one' register (the Rachel-line beloved-late-born). Commentary at v 8.
