## Reviewer attention

### 1. `aman-tzedakah-counted-as-righteousness` (v 6)

The highest reception-weight verse in the chapter and one of the
three most-quoted Hebrew Bible verses in Pauline theology. The
Reviewer should verify that:

- The translated English (*and he believed in YHWH; and he reckoned
  it to him for righteousness*, matching ASV) does not preempt the
  Pauline-Reformation justification-by-faith superstructure. It does
  not; the language carries the Hebrew *aman*-trust and the *chashav*
  accounting register without forensic-imputation language.
- The glossary entry's rationale presents the downstream Pauline
  reception (Rom 4:3, 4:9, 4:22; Gal 3:6; James 2:23; the Reformation
  superstructure) as *flagged but not preempted*. The entry cites
  Moberly 1992, Sanders 1977, Dunn 1988, Wright 2013, Westerholm
  2004, Cranfield 1975 — the full range of Pauline-reception
  scholarship without committing to a position.
- The James 2:23 *opposite-use* of the same verse is acknowledged.
  The Pauline-Jamesian split on the verse is itself a flag of
  reception complexity.
- The commentary at v 6 maintains the same posture: the Hebrew is
  read; the Pauline reception is named as downstream Greek-and-Latin
  work; the Reformation superstructure is named as downstream of the
  Pauline reading.

The entry was written to comply with hard rule 6 (commentary surfaces
lexical evidence; it does not editorialise). The Reviewer should
confirm that the comply is genuine and not merely formal.

### 2. `tanur-ashan-lappid-esh-theophany` (v 17)

High-lens-leverage. The chapter's keystone concrete-sensory divine
appearance, with smoke-and-fire imagery whose Wheel of Heaven reading
will be developed at length in long-form articles and wiki material
downstream. The Reviewer should verify that:

- The translated English (*a smoking furnace, and a flaming torch
  that passed between these pieces*, matching ASV) does not insert a
  Wheel of Heaven cosmological reading. It does not; the imagery is
  preserved literally.
- The glossary entry stays in mainstream scholarly register. The
  cited authorities are Westermann, Wenham, von Rad, Sarna, Hamilton
  — the modern critical Genesis-commentary consensus. The
  cross-references (Exod 19:18 Sinai, Exod 13:21 pillar-of-fire, Isa
  6:4, Ezek 1) are all direct documentary evidence. No Wheel of
  Heaven cosmological interpretation is offered.
- The unilateral-divine-passage reading (Yahweh alone passes between
  the pieces while Abram is in *tardemah*) is presented as scholarly
  consensus, which it is.
- The commentary at v 17 maintains the same posture. The lens-relevant
  material is summarized but not editorialized; the developed Wheel of
  Heaven reading is explicitly deferred to wiki and long-form
  material.

### 3. `400-years-affliction-prophecy` (vv 13, 14, 16)

The Reviewer should verify the entry presents the 400 / 430 /
four-generation chronology dispute without taking a definitive
position. The entry catalogues the four chronological witnesses (Gen
15:13's 400; Gen 15:16's four generations; MT Exod 12:40's 430 in
Mitsrayim; SP/LXX Exod 12:40's 430 split across Canaan and
Mitsrayim), the NT receptions (Acts 7:6's 400; Gal 3:17's 430), and
the three harmonization strategies (Sarna's round-number reading;
the Levitical-genealogical reading; the discrepant-tradition
reading). The entry does not adjudicate; it preserves the dispute
and lets the Reviewer / human sign-off decide whether the
Wheel of Heaven project commits to a chronology.

### 4. `brit-bein-ha-betarim` (vv 9-11, 17-18)

The Reviewer should verify the ANE parallel citations are accurate.
The cited material:

- **Mari archives, ARM II 37 and ARM II 80** — Old Babylonian,
  eighteenth century BCE; sacrificial-animal bisection in
  covenant-ratification contexts; *qatāl ḫayyāri* (killing of an
  ass-foal) ritual idiom.
- **Alalakh tablets AT 1 and AT 76** — Middle / Late Bronze Age;
  parallel rites.
- **Sefire Aramaic treaties KAI 222-224** — eighth century BCE,
  Bar-Ga'yah of KTK and Mati'el of Arpad; explicit curse-formula
  preserved (*kma zy yqd 'gl' znh*). Fitzmyer 1995 is the standard
  edition.
- **Tell Fakhariyeh** ninth-century Aramaic inscription, covenant
  vocabulary.
- **Mesha stele** — covenant-curse vocabulary in West-Semitic
  register.
- **Esarhaddon vassal-treaties** — Neo-Assyrian developed form with
  explicit animal-bisection imagery.
- **Modern critical authorities**: Weinfeld 1970 *JAOS* 90;
  Mendenhall 1955; Hillers 1969; McCarthy 1978; Fitzmyer 1995.

The Reviewer is encouraged to spot-check the Mari ARM II 37 and II 80
references against Charpin's standard editions of the Mari archives,
the Alalakh AT 1 and AT 76 references against Wiseman's *The Alalakh
Tablets* (1953), and the Sefire references against Fitzmyer's 1995
edition. The Jer 34:18-20 cross-reference is direct in-Bible
material and can be verified at the verse itself.
