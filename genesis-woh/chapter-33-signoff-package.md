## The chapter's character

Gen 33 closes the Esau-Jacob arc that opened at Gen 25's birthright sale and ran through Gen 27's blessing-deception and Gen 32's Penuel wrestling. Four signature moments:

- **v 4** — the *puncta extraordinaria* over *va-yishaqehu* (the second of the 15 canonical *nequdot* in Genesis after Gen 18:9's *elav*); the kiss-or-bite rabbinic tradition
- **v 10** — *ra'iti panekha ki-r'ot p'nei Elohim* — the explicit Esav-reunion echo of the Penuel *panim el panim* (Gen 32:31)
- **v 17/18/20** — three name-aetiologies in close succession: Sukkot (booths), Shalem (or "in peace"), and the altar **El Elohei Yisra'el** — the fifth and final patriarchal El-compound (after El Elyon, El Roi, El Shaddai, YHWH El Olam)
- **v 19** — the patriarchal land-acquisition pattern (Machpelah → Shechem → Aravnah) and the critical Hivite-not-Hittite distinction
