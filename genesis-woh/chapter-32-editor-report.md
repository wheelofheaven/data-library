## Summary

Genesis 32 is one of the most lens-relevant and load-bearing theophany-chapters in the Hebrew Bible. The chapter contains: (a) the Machanayim divine-messenger encounter at the inbound boundary of the Promised Land (matching the outbound Bethel-ladder vision at Gen 28); (b) Ya'aqov's tribute-strategy with its four-fold *minchah* deployment and its v 21 cultic-vocabulary import; (c) the Yabboq-ford wrestling with the unnamed *ish*; (d) the renaming to Yisra'el with its grammatically-ambiguous in-text etymology; (e) the *Peni'el* / *panim el panim* survived-theophany formula — foundational for HB theophany-theology; (f) the *gid ha-nasheh* dietary-rule aetiology — the only HB dietary-prohibition grounded in a narrator's-time aetiology rather than a divine-command.

### `yisrael-name-aetiology-sarah-im-elohim` (overlay)

**Source:** *ki-sarita im-Elohim v-im-anashim va-tukhal* (Gen 32:29)
**WoH choice:** *for you have striven with Elohim and with men, and have prevailed*
**Claim type:** `direct`
**Why load-bearing:** The verse contains (a) the patriarchal-corpus's second renaming; (b) the explicit in-text etymology of the people-name *Yisra'el* — one of the HB's most-significant proper-names; (c) a grammatically-ambiguous etymology that has generated extensive scholarly discussion.
**The etymology-dispute:** The in-text etymology unambiguously makes **the patriarch** the subject of the striving (Ya'aqov has striven). But the grammatical-form of the name *Yisra'el* with the *yi-* prefix is more naturally read as the imperfect verbal form with **El as the subject** (*El strives*) — the theophoric-naming pattern that recurs across HB proper-names. Modern critical scholarship (Cross *CMHE* 1973 pp. 44-45; HALOT s.v. *Yisra'el*) treats the in-text etymology as a post-facto folk-etymology. Five etymological positions are documented in the glossary entry.
**Reviewer action requested:** Confirm that the in-text etymology surface-reading is preserved in the translation while the five-position scholarly landscape is documented in the glossary. Consider whether `yisrael-name-aetiology-sarah-im-elohim` should be promoted from overlay to central glossary when Hos 12:4-5 / Isa 49 / Exod 4 are translated (these chapters develop the name-etymology across the canon).

### `peniel-panim-el-panim-divine-face-encounter` (central)

**Source:** *Peni'el / panim el panim / va-tinatzel nafshi* (Gen 32:31)
**WoH choice:** *Peni'el / face to face / my life is preserved*
**Claim type:** `direct`
**Why load-bearing:** The verse is the foundational HB instance of a human-survived face-to-face encounter with Elohim. The cross-corpus *panim el panim* formula recurs at Exod 33:11 (Moshe), Deut 5:4 (the Sinai-people), Deut 34:10 (the defining-characteristic of Mosaic uniqueness), Judg 6:22 (Gid'on), and elsewhere. The *va-tinatzel nafshi* survived-theophany clause is foundational for the HB theology that direct-divine-visibility is fatal-by-default (Exod 33:20). The Exod 33:11 / 33:20 tension within a single chapter is one of the HB's most-discussed theophany-cruxes.
**Reviewer action requested:** Confirm that the cross-corpus survived-theophany cluster (Gen 16:13; Gen 32:31; Exod 33:11; Judg 6:22; Judg 13:22; Isa 6:5; Deut 5:4; Deut 34:10) is correctly documented in the glossary entry. The entry's *appliesTo* is anchored at GEN-WOH-32:31 and 32:32; cross-corpus expansion will follow as those chapters are translated.

### `gid-ha-nasheh-sciatic-nerve-dietary-rule` (overlay)

**Source:** *gid ha-nasheh* (Gen 32:33)
**WoH choice:** *the sinew of the thigh-vein* (ASV-baseline, preserving the *nasheh* hapax)
**Claim type:** `direct`
**Why load-bearing:** The verse is **the only HB occurrence of the *gid ha-nasheh* dietary-rule aetiology** — and **the only HB dietary-rule whose origin is a narrator's-time aetiology rather than a divine-command**. Every other HB kashrut-rule (Lev 11; Deut 14; Gen 9:4; Exod 23:19 / 34:26 / Deut 14:21) is anchored to a divine-command formula. The *gid ha-nasheh* alone is grounded in a patriarchal-narrative-precedent. The categorical-distinction is editorially load-bearing.
**Post-biblical halakhic development:** The verse is extensively elaborated in m. Chullin 7; b. Chullin 89b-103b; Sifre to Deut 14; Maimonides Hilkhot Ma'akhalot Asurot 8; Shulchan Arukh Yoreh De'ah 65. The rabbinic identification of the *nasheh* as the sciatic nerve drives the *nikkur* procedure operative in current orthodox-Jewish butchery.
**Reviewer action requested:** Confirm that the *nasheh* hapax is preserved via the *thigh-vein* gloss (rather than over-translated as *sciatic nerve*). Confirm that the only-narrator-derived-kashrut-rule documentary status is correctly presented as a `direct` claim. The entry is overlay-resident because the lemma is text-specific; promotion to central is not warranted.
