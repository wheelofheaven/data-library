## Items flagged for Reviewer attention

### `rav-yaavod-tzair-younger-displaces-elder` — NT reception apparatus

The Pauline Rom 9:10-13 reception is treated extensively in both the
glossary entry rationale and the v 23 commentary. Two interpretive
traditions are documented (Calvinist-predestinarian; Arminian /
Wesleyan / modern Catholic corporate-peoples) and the WoH apparatus
explicitly does not adjudicate. Reviewer should confirm that the
flagged-not-preempted treatment is the right level of engagement —
the alternative would be to push the reception deeper into the
apparatus (i.e., note the existence of the reception but not
characterize the Calvinist / Arminian split). The current treatment
gives more reception-history detail than typical for this glossary.

### `bekhorah-birthright-firstborn-status` — Nuzi parallels with critical caution

Speiser's 1964 *AB Genesis* Nuzi-parallel argument is one of the most-
cited HB / ANE-legal comparisons; the modern critical retreat from the
overdrawn parallels (Selman 1976; Van Seters 1975; Greengus 1975;
Eichler 1989) is the standard scholarly correction. The entry presents
both Speiser's argument and the critical caution. Reviewer should
confirm that paired-presentation is appropriate — the alternative would
be to either omit the Speiser argument entirely (cleaner but loses
historical-scholarly-history context) or omit the critical caution
(more confident but methodologically problematic). The current treatment
follows the modern critical consensus presentation in Sarna, Westermann,
and the recent commentaries.

### `va-yivez-esav-et-ha-bekhorah-despising-birthright` — Hebrews 12:16-17 and rabbinic reception

Two distinct reception traditions are documented:
- **NT Hebrews 12:16-17** (Esav as *bebēlos* / profane) with patristic
  extension across Origen, Augustine, Calvin, and the Lectionary
  tradition pairing Gen 25:31-34 with Heb 12:16-17 in Christian
  liturgical use.
- **Rabbinic Genesis Rabbah 63:11 / b. Bava Batra 16b** (the five-sins
  expansion: violation of betrothed maiden, murder, denial of
  resurrection, denial of providence, despising the birthright).

Both reception traditions are flagged-not-preempted. The modern critical
narrower reading (appetite-over-promise pattern; Sarna / Westermann /
Wenham / Hamilton / Alter / Speiser) is presented as the lexical-
philological consensus alongside the reception-history. Reviewer should
confirm the three-way presentation (NT reception / rabbinic reception /
modern critical reading) is the right level.

### `ish-tam-ish-sadeh-jacob-esau-contrast` — `tam` four-reading dispute

The lexeme *tam* admits four readings: R1 quiet (ASV/dominant; chosen),
R2 plain (KJV), R3 blameless (modern critical), R4 naive (Fokkelman
minority). The R3 reading creates an immediate narrative-tension with
the immediately-following supplant-acts and is theologically the most-
loaded; Wenham *WBC* explicitly takes R3. The WoH translation follows
R1 (ASV-baseline) as the dominant — a conservative editorial choice.
Reviewer should confirm: (a) is R1 the right baseline, or should the
translation move to R3 (which would make the Ya'aqov-character's
internal-tension more visible in the English)? The argument for R1: it
matches the ASV-baseline practice across the WoH project and avoids
foregrounding the moral-integrity reading that creates the narrative-
tension only on a particular reading. The argument for R3: the lexical-
family is *tamim* (Gen 17:1 Avraham, Gen 6:9 Noah, Job 1:1) and the
covenantal-ethical register is the lexeme's primary HB reach. The
glossary entry preserves both readings; the question is whether the
main-text English follows R1 or R3.

### `darash-yhwh-seeking-oracle` — first HB occurrence and 1 Sam 9:9

The Gen 25:22 verse is the lexically-foundational instance of the
*darash YHWH* tradition that runs across the entire HB historiographic
and prophetic corpus. The cross-corpus reach (Exodus, Samuel, Kings,
Chronicles, Jeremiah, Ezekiel) is substantial, and the entry necessarily
sketches the cross-corpus tradition. Reviewer should confirm: is the
cross-corpus apparatus appropriate for a Genesis-25 entry, or should the
cross-corpus material be deferred to future entries when the relevant
verses are translated? The entry follows the project's established
practice (cf. `atar-l-yhwh-intercession` companion entry, `tamim-walk-
before-god-covenant` Gen 17:1 entry, `pere-adam-wild-ass-prophecy` Gen
16:12 entry) of documenting the cross-corpus reach at the foundational-
HB-instance, with cross-references for future entries to back-link.
