# Chapter 48 Editor Report

Genesis 48 is the deathbed-adoption-blessing chapter: the formal
institutional act by which Yosef becomes two through his sons
(Ephraim and Menasheh elevated to tribal-eponym status equal to
Re'uven and Shim'on); the El-Shaddai-at-Luz covenant-memory recital;
the *ha-mal'akh ha-go'el oti mi-kol-ra* unified-theophanic-redeemer
formula (the chapter's theological signature); the deliberate
hand-crossing (*sikkel et-yadav*) reversing the firstborn-order;
the *yesimkha Elohim ke-Ephrayim ve-khi-Menasheh* liturgical-
blessing formula (the only Genesis blessing-formula to become a
fixed Jewish-liturgical formula); the *shekhem achad* polysemy and
historical-redactional crux. Structurally the chapter sits between
the post-settlement burial-oath chapter (Gen 47) and the deathbed-
blessings of the twelve sons (Gen 49) → Ya'aqov's death and burial
(Gen 50); the chapter is the cycle's institutional-reorganization
move (the twelve-tribe federation acquires its final structure here).

The chapter required substantial central-glossary investment: **eight
new central entries** plus **ten central appliesTo extensions**
(including three rationale extensions) plus **substantial overlay
investment**: **fourteen new overlay entries** plus **one
bidirectional central-overlay sibling-pair** (the project's **sixth**
such pair). All 19 `editorial_questions[]` were resolved without
escalation. No `claim_type=speculative` entries are advanced.

The translator's draft was strong throughout; the editorial pass
resolved every flagged item via glossary entry, appliesTo extension,
or commentary cross-reference. New entries sit at `direct` (20) or
`inferred` (3, the literary-observation entries on the Rachel-recall,
the name-shift pattern, and the adoption-on-the-knees ritual). The
translation surface stays ASV-baseline 1901 with WoH proper-name
transliteration; no WoH-distinctive synthesis appears on the surface
or in any rationale.

## Speculative entries requiring sign-off

**None.** No `claim_type=speculative` entries are advanced in this

The chapter's two principal lens-risk concentrations were handled
with strict named-scholarly-literary register:

(a) **The *ha-mal'akh ha-go'el oti mi-kol-ra* unified-theophanic-
redeemer formula (v 16).** The central `ha-mal-akh-ha-go-el-mi-kol-
ra-unified-theophanic-redeemer-formula` documents the formula as
the chapter's theological signature, compressing the cycle's prior
theophanic moments (Gen 28:13-15 the ladder; Gen 31:11-13 the dream-
revelation with the *mal'akh ha-Elohim* / *ha-El Beit-El* identification;
Gen 32:25-30 the wrestling-stranger; Gen 35:9-15 the El Shaddai at
Luz) into a single divine-naming construction. The *mal'akh* /
Elohim identification is documented through named scholarship (A.
Rofé *Mal'akhei Elohim* 1979 the standard treatment; G. von Rad
*Genesis* OTL on the proto-monotheistic compromise reading; W.
Brueggemann *Genesis* Interpretation on the personalist-relational
divine-naming; N. Sarna *JPS Genesis*; V. P. Hamilton *NICOT Genesis
18-50*; B. Childs on the *go'el* kinsman-language; J. Levenson; C.
Westermann; M. S. Smith *The Origins of Biblical Monotheism*; S. A.
Meier *The Messenger in the Ancient Semitic World* HSM 45, 1988; K.
Schmid; R. Hess; with named-but-non-adjudicated reception-tradition
notes on J. T. Christensen *The Angel of the Lord* 2001 for the
patristic-and-medieval Christian Christophany-tradition). The *go'el*
kinsman-redeemer vocabulary is documented across the cross-corpus
Job 19:25 / Isa 41-66 / Ruth 4 distribution. **STRICT lens-discipline
maintained throughout**: NO Christian 'Angel of the Lord' /
Christophany / Trinitarian-typology pre-resolution; NO WoH
*mal'akh*-as-'messenger-from-a-designer-civilization' synthesis. The
translation surface remains ASV-baseline 'the messenger who hath
redeemed me from all evil'; the rationale is in named-scholarship
register throughout.

(b) **The firstborn-reversal pattern explicit articulation (v 19) +
the *melo ha-goyim* Rom 11:25 reception (v 19).** The existing
central `firstborn-priority-reversal-irony` (which already covered
GEN-48:13-20 in its appliesTo) received a rationale-extension marking
v 19 as the pattern's *explicit articulation locus* — the only verse
where the principle is verbally stated on the patriarch's own lips.
The pattern's broader theological-architectural significance is
documented through named scholarship (von Rad on patriarchal
election-theology; J. Levenson *The Death and Resurrection of the
Beloved Son* 1993; F. E. Greenspahn *When Brothers Dwell Together*
1994). The overlay `melo-ha-goyim-fullness-of-nations-rom-11-
reception-gen48` documents the Rom 11:25 NT-citation reception as
named-scholarship-apparatus only (Cranfield, Dunn, Moo; the post-
Holocaust Christian-Jewish-dialogue documentation: Stendahl; the
recent Jewish-readings-of-Paul: Boyarin, Nanos). **STRICT lens-
discipline**: NO Christian-theological supersession adjudication;
NO WoH supersession-spin. The translation surface remains ASV-
baseline 'a multitude of nations'.

(c) **The *shekhem achad* polysemy and historical-redactional crux
(v 22).** The central `shekhem-achad-polysemy-historical-redactional-
crux` documents the *shekhem* polysemy (shoulder / portion / Shekhem-
city) and the three named-scholarship reading-camps on the *asher
laqachti mi-yad ha-Emori* historical-redactional crux (Skinner /
Speiser / Friedman / Carr source-critical fragmentary reading;
Cassuto / Hamilton / Wenham / Ramban harmonizing readings; Westermann
/ von Rad irreducibly-difficult position). NO WoH ANE-conquest
synthesis; NO theological adjudication on the source-critical /
harmonizing / irreducibly-difficult reading-divide. The translation
surface remains ASV-baseline 'one portion above thy brethren' (the
lexical-baseline reading), without precluding the Shekhem-pun reading.

(d) **The patriarchal name-bequest and Friday-night liturgical-
blessing-formula receptions (vv 16, 20).** The Birkat ha-Banim
liturgical-tradition is documented through named-rabbinic-and-
medieval-liturgical scholarship (Sarna, Heinemann, Reif, Idelsohn,
Hoffmann; the *Machzor Vitry* and the broader medieval-liturgical-
collections; the parallel daughter-blessing tradition). The patriarchal-
name-bequest formula's NT Acts 15:17 reception is named-but-not-
adjudicated. NO Christian-theological supersession adjudication;
NO WoH liturgical-spin.
