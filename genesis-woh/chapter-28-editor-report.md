# Chapter 28 Editor Report

## Summary

Genesis 28 — Yitzchaq's commissioning blessing, Esav's third wife, and
Ya'aqov's ladder-vision at Beit-El — is one of the highest-lens-leverage
chapters in the Jacob cycle and one of the most-discussed theophany
scenes in the Hebrew Bible. The Translator passed clean with 22 verses,
all English filled, 16 editorial_questions flagged, 4 verses with
glossaryRefs. Editor cleared all 16 editorial_questions, added 11 new
glossary entries (one departing from ASV: `sulam-stairway-or-ladder`),
extended appliesTo on 6 existing entries, and wrote substantial
commentary on 12 verses including the four mandatory-major lens-loci
(vv 12, 13, 17, 18; plus vv 20-22 vow-and-tithe).

All 11 new entries are `claim_type: direct`. **No speculative entries.**
The chapter's wider lens-distinctive synthesis (the *sulam* as
physical-staircase / the *matzevah* as physical stone-marker of a
physical divine-encounter-site / the *sha'ar ha-shamayim* as physical
gate-of-the-heavens architecture) is preserved for the wiki and does
not enter either the translated English or the glossary apparatus.

Translation advanced from `1.0.0-draft` / `draft` to
`1.0.0-rc1` / `editor-review`. Glossary bumped 2.25.0 → 2.26.0
(semver-minor, additions-only).

## Direct entries flagged for Reviewer attention

These are not speculative, but each carries one or more editorial
decisions the Reviewer should explicitly endorse.

### `sulam-stairway-or-ladder`

**Source:** סֻלָּם (hapax legomenon, Gen 28:12 only)
**WoH choice:** *stairway* (modern critical preference per Houtman 1977
*VT* 27, Sarna, Speiser, Hamilton, Westermann)
**ASV departure:** ASV / KJV / NRSV / NJPS all read *ladder*; the WoH
default *stairway* is a deliberate translation-text departure
**Why flagged:** The reading is mainstream modern-critical-consensus
but is the only chapter-28 translation-text divergence from ASV.
**Recommendation:** Ship. The departure is editorially-motivated by the
chapter's Akkadian-ziggurat-architecture intertext (*simmiltu ša šamê*
+ *bab-ili* + Gen 11 Babel-echo) which the *ladder* rendering loses.
Houtman 1977 supplies the philological grounds; the four standard
modern commentaries (Sarna, Speiser, Hamilton, Westermann) all adopt
*stairway* or *staircase*.

### `beit-elohim-shaar-ha-shamayim-cultic-formula`

**Source:** אֵין זֶה כִּי אִם־בֵּית אֱלֹהִים וְזֶה שַׁעַר הַשָּׁמָיִם
(v 17)
**WoH choice:** *house of Elohim / gate of the heavens* (preserving
*Elohim* transliteration and the literal-plural *ha-shamayim*)
**Why flagged:** The *sha'ar ha-shamayim* = Akkadian *bab-ili* (gate of
god) parallel is one of the most editorially-distinctive ANE-comparative
findings in Genesis. The Gen 11 Babel-narrative anti-intertext (where
Babel-builders fail to build the gate-to-heaven and are scattered;
Ya'aqov discovers the gate-to-heaven and is blessed) is load-bearing
for the chapter's wider narrative-architecture.
**NT and rabbinic receptions flagged-not-preempted:** John 1:51 (the
Son-of-Man Christology of the gateway); Genesis Rabbah 69:7 (the
Bethel-as-proto-Temple Mount reading). Both are documentary
post-biblical receptions and are flagged in the entry's rationale; the
WoH translation does not enter either reading.
**Recommendation:** Ship.

### `matzevah-sacred-pillar-patriarchal-religion`

**Source:** מַצֵּבָה (vv 18, 22)
**WoH choice:** *pillar* (ASV-baseline; the patriarchal-El-religion /
Deuteronomic-prohibition tension surfaced in apparatus, not in English)
**Why flagged:** This is one of the HB's most editorially-load-bearing
cultic-history doublets (the patriarchs build the very cultic-objects
the later legislation absolutely prohibits — Gen 28:18 vs Deut 16:22).
The entry adopts the Alt 1929 / Cross 1973 / Smith 2001 / Mettinger
1995 reading of the matzevah as a patriarchal-El-religion relic; this
is mainstream modern-critical-consensus but is also the textual
foundation for the WoH lens-distinctive matzevah-as-physical-stone-marker
reading (reserved for the wiki).
**Recommendation:** Ship. The translation text remains conservative
(*pillar*, ASV); the lens-relevant scholarly framework is documented
in the apparatus.

### `malakhei-elohim-ascending-descending`

**Source:** מַלְאֲכֵי אֱלֹהִים עֹלִים וְיֹרְדִים בּוֹ (v 12)
**WoH choice:** *messengers of Elohim ascending and descending on it*
(preserving the plural construct, the *Elohim* transliteration, and the
participle word-order)
**claim_type:** `direct` (Editor's call — see entry rationale's discussion
of the `direct` vs `inferred` boundary)
**Why flagged:** The Reviewer brief offered `direct` or `inferred` as
Editor's call. Editor selected `direct` because the entry makes no
synthetic claim about *what* the messengers are; it documents the
plural-construct grammatical fact, the patriarchal-corpus uniqueness,
the E-document immanence-mediation pattern (mainstream consensus), and
the NT John 1:51 + rabbinic Gen Rab 68:12 receptions (documentary).
The lens-distinctive ontological reading is reserved for the wiki and
is not preempted in the entry.
**Recommendation:** Ship as `direct`. Reviewer may downgrade to
`inferred` if she judges the E-document immanence-mediation pattern
as inference-rather-than-direct.

### `yaakov-vow-tithe-promise`

**Source:** וַיִּדַּר יַעֲקֹב נֶדֶר ... עַשֵּׂר אֲעַשְּׂרֶנּוּ לָךְ
(vv 20-22)
**WoH choice:** *vowed a vow ... I will surely give a tenth* (preserving
both cognate-accusatives)
**Why flagged:** The entry covers two HB-first-occurrences in a single
pericope — the first explicit *neder* (vow) and the second pre-Sinai
*ma'aser* (tithe). The pericope establishes the cross-corpus
tithe-tradition that develops across Lev 27, Num 18, Deut 12-14, 26,
and Mal 3:8-10, with downstream NT Heb 7:1-10 reception. The
foundational status makes the entry load-bearing for any future tithe-
or vow-related glossary work.
**Recommendation:** Ship.

   *stairway* at v 12** (where ASV reads *ladder*). All other
   translation-text choices preserve ASV-baseline or the closest
   modern-critical-consensus rendering. The departure is the chapter's
   single concentrated lens-relevant decision and is documented above.

   wiki-deferred readings**: (a) the *sulam* as physical
   ziggurat-staircase / cosmic-mediation-platform; (b) the *matzevah*

   the *sha'ar ha-shamayim* as physical gate-of-heavens architecture.
   All three are preserved for the wiki. The glossary entries surface

   `malakhei-elohim-ascending-descending` (John 1:51; Gen Rab 68:12;
   b. Chullin 91b), and `beit-elohim-shaar-ha-shamayim-cultic-formula`
   (John 1:51; Gen Rab 69:7). The flagged-not-preempted policy is
   consistent with prior chapters.
