# Chapter 45 Editor Report

Genesis 45 is the cycle's recognition-and-reconciliation chapter and
the explicit theological-articulation-point: Yosef's three-fold
providential-sending formula at vv 5, 7, 8 is the cycle's interpretive
payload, and the chapter closes the chayah-keyword arc (v 5 *le-michyah*
/ v 7 *u-le-hachayot* / v 27 *va-techi ruach Ya'aqov*) at the literal
revival of the patriarch's spirit. The chapter has the highest
single-chapter central-glossary-load of the cycle so far: six new
central entries (including the project's third central-to-central
sibling-link), one central appliesTo extension, sixteen new overlay
entries (including three bidirectional sibling-pair links to existing
overlays), and one overlay appliesTo extension.

The translator's draft was uniformly strong; the editorial pass
resolved all 27 editorial_questions[] without escalation. No
*claim_type=speculative* entries are advanced. All new entries are
`direct` (16) or `inferred` (6), per the chapter's mainstream-scholarly
substance. The translation surface stays ASV-baseline 1901 with WoH
proper-name transliteration; no WoH-distinctive synthesis appears on
the surface or in any rationale.

## Speculative entries requiring sign-off

**None.** No `claim_type=speculative` entries are advanced in this
pass. Every new entry sits at `direct` (anchored in well-documented
cross-corpus lexical-distribution or grammatical fact) or `inferred`
