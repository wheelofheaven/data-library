## Chapter's signature additions

### `el-shaddai` (load-bearing #1)

First HB occurrence of the divine name at v 1; recurs at the P-document patriarchal occurrences (Gen 28:3, 35:11, 43:14, 48:3, 49:25). **Exod 6:3 is the P-document's programmatic harmonization statement** — "I appeared to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob as *El Shaddai*, but by my name *YHWH* I was not known to them." The entry presents **all three modern critical etymologies in genuine parallel**:
- (i) *shadad* "destroy / be powerful" (traditional; LXX *pantokrator*, Vulgate *omnipotens*)
- (ii) Akkadian *šadû* "mountain" — "El of the mountain" (Cross 1973 *CMHE* pp. 52-60, Albright *JBL* 54 [1935])
- (iii) West-Semitic *Šaddai* class-epithet, with the **Deir 'Alla *shdyn*** as direct epigraphic evidence (Ouellette *RB* 76, Hess *Israelite Religions* 2007)

WoH posture stays in apparatus. Reviewer-verified.

### `brit-milah-circumcision-covenant` (load-bearing #2)

The chapter's high-reception-sensitivity entry. Reviewer-verified to be:
- Anchored in mainstream HB-scholarship register (Sasson 1966 *JBL*, Bergmann 1968, Propp *AB Exodus* 1999 on *chatan damim*, Cohen 1996 *Beginnings of Jewishness*)
- ANE parallels accurate (Egyptian circumcision per Herodotus II.36-37, Sixth Dynasty Saqqara reliefs)
- Cross-corpus reach comprehensive (Gen 21:4, 34, Exod 4:24-26, 12:43-49, Lev 12:3, Josh 5:2-9, 1 Sam 17:26/36, Jer 9:24-25, Ezek 44:7-9)

- Pauline and rabbinic receptions treated in parallel; neither favored
