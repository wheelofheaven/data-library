# Chapter 18 Editor Report

Genesis 18 — The three visitors at Mamre and Avraham's intercession for Sodom.

The Editor has resolved all twelve `editorial_questions[]` (now empty),
added eleven new glossary entries to the central translation glossary,
written substantial commentary on the eight keystone verses, and advanced
the chapter to `editor-review` / `1.0.0-rc1` / glossary pin `2.15.0`.

This chapter is the highest-lens-leverage theophany in the Abraham cycle.
The Editor has held strict accuracy-above-lens discipline: no
project-specific WoH synthesis has been injected into the glossary
entries or per-verse commentary. The lens stays in the apparatus *only*

on the fluidity model; the recurring divine-descent pattern at Gen 11:5,
18:21, Exod 3:8 as J-source spatial-anthropomorphism). Project-specific
synthesis is reserved for the wiki and long-form material downstream.

## Speculative entries requiring sign-off

None. All eleven new glossary entries are `claim_type=direct`.

The Editor's reasoning on the borderline calls:

### `divine-descent-to-investigate-pattern`

The brief allowed Editor's discretion between `direct` and `inferred`.
The Editor's call: **direct**. Justification: (a) the verb *yarad* in
cohortative or imperfect form at Gen 11:5, 11:7, 18:21, Exod 3:8 is
grammatically explicit at every cited verse; (b) the verbal parallels
between the verses are verbatim or near-verbatim and lexically
verifiable; (c) the pattern-as-recurring-J-source-formula reading is
mainstream HB scholarship documented in Westermann 1984/1985, Sarna
1989, Niditch 1985, and Hamori 2008. The entry's claim is purely
philological-lexical (the formula recurs, with these features, at these
verses). The lens-relevance is acknowledged in the chapter-level
commentary but the glossary entry itself stays in lexical-philological
register.

### `shloshah-anashim-three-visitors-theophany`

The brief identified this as THE keystone entry. The Editor's call:
**direct**. Justification: the entry's central claim is the
*lexical-grammatical fact* of the singular-plural oscillation, which is
grammatically explicit at every cited verse. The four modern critical
positions on the oscillation (Westermann's J-document theophany,
Sarna's literary-effect, Hamori's anthropomorphic realism, Sommer's
fluidity model, Letellier's chiastic Mamre/Sodom diptych) are presented
in parallel; *none is preempted*; the WoH translation commits to none.
The Christian reception (Heb 13:2 hospitality, Augustine *De Trinitate*
II.10-11, the Rublev Trinity icon of 1411) is documented as downstream
and explicitly *not preempted in the translation*. The entry rationale
reads as mainstream-scholarly throughout.

### `divine-eating-anthropomorphism`

Hamori 2008 *When Gods Were Men*, chs 3-4, is the load-bearing modern
scholarly citation. The entry develops the contrast between the J-
source's unhedged eating at Gen 18:8 / 19:3 and the later strata's
glosses (Judg 13:15-20 explicit refusal; Tob 12:19 LXX harmonizing).
The entry stays in mainstream HB-scholarship register: the
*anthropomorphic realism* reading is presented as Hamori's position,
with Sommer 2009 *The Bodies of God* as the related but distinct
*fluidity model*. No project-specific extension.

### `divine-descent-to-investigate-pattern`

claim_type set to **direct** (Editor's call). The lens-relevance is
significant — the *divine investigation* move maps onto WoH's
engagement with the texts — but the entry itself stays in mainstream
scholarly register: the recurring pattern across Gen 11:5, 11:7, 18:21,
Exod 3:8, Exod 19:11/18/20 is documented; the Babel-Sodom verbal
parallel is editorially deliberate and is treated as such in Sarna 1989
and Westermann 1984. The closing paragraph reads:
"Anthropomorphic realism — entry-level scholarly register only. The
Gen 18:21 formula is one of the patriarchal narratives' clearest
examples of the J-source's *spatial anthropomorphism*: YHWH is not
omniscient-from-above but learns by *going down to see*. The reading is
preserved in mainstream HB scholarship; the WoH translation makes no
further interpretive move beyond the established scholarly register."
Reviewer should verify this discipline holds.

### `abraham-intercession-bargaining`

The foundational-justice-formula framing is grounded in:

- Brueggemann *Genesis* (Interpretation) 1982 — the load-bearing modern
  treatment of the moral-theological significance
- Brueggemann *Theology of the Old Testament* 1997, pp. 384-389 — the
  *anti-divine-injustice* prophetic register
- Childs *Old Testament Theology in a Canonical Context* 1985 on
  intercession as a theological category
- Alter *Genesis* 1996, pp. 81-84 — the literary-rhetorical climax-of-
  mercy device

The entry consolidates the v 25 universal-judge formula and the
descending-bargaining sequence (50→45→40→30→20→10) into a single
intercession-pericope entry, with cross-references to the related
entries `tzedakah-u-mishpat-justice-pair` (v 19), `afar-va-efer-
creatural-humility` (v 27), and `zaaqah-outcry-of-the-oppressed` (vv
20-21). The cross-corpus intercession-tradition (Moses, Samuel, Amos,
Ezekiel, Job 42) is documented.

### `puncta-extraordinaria-v9-elav`

The Masorah tradition and modern critical interpretation are presented
in parallel:

- **Traditional rabbinic reading** — b. Bava Metzia 87a; Bereshit
  Rabbah 48:15; *Sofrim* 6:3. Two main interpretations (the visitors-
  knew-Sarah reading; the asked-each-other reading) documented.
- **Modern text-critical reading** — Tov 2012 §6.B.5; McCarthy 1981;
  Yeivin 1980 §80-81; Würthwein 1995; Ginsburg 1897.

The standard list of fifteen *puncta extraordinaria* is provided. The
Gen 18:9 case is unusual in that all four letters of *elav* are dotted
simultaneously; the entry discusses this and notes that LXX, Samaritan
Pentateuch, Vulgate, and Targumim all preserve *elav*, weakening the
text-critical (omission-variant) interpretation in this specific case
while preserving the editorial-flag interpretation as the simpler
default for the *puncta extraordinaria* class as a whole.
