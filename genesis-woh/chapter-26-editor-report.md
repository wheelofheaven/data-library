## Items for Reviewer attention (non-speculative)

### `mishmeret-mitzvot-chuqim-torot-fourfold-covenant`

**Source:** *va-yishmor mishmarti mitzvotai chuqotai v-torotai* (Gen
26:5)

**WoH choice:** *kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and
my laws* (ASV-baseline four-noun rendering preserved; source-critical
anomaly surfaced in apparatus)

**Why this needs attention:** The verse is the chapter's clearest
documentary-source signal. The Sinaitic-retrojection / P-or-late-D
layer reading is mainstream modern-critical-consensus (Wellhausen
1885; von Rad 1956; Westermann 1985; Sarna 1989; Wenham 1994;
Hamilton 1995; Alter 2004; Friedman 1987, 2003), but the rabbinic-
traditional Avraham-observed-Torah-by-anticipation reading (m.
Qidd. 4:14; b. Yoma 28b; Gen Rab 64:4; Rashi) is editorially
significant for the reception history and is flagged-not-preempted
in the rationale. Reviewer should confirm the balance is right —
that the modern critical reading is presented as the philological-
historical position without dismissing the rabbinic-traditional
reading as merely interpretive amplification.

**Recommendation:** Ship.

### `al-tira-fear-not-divine-encouragement-formula`

**Source:** *al-tira ki-itkha anokhi* (Gen 26:24)

**WoH choice:** *do not fear, for I am with you* (ASV-baseline; the
cross-corpus distribution and ANE context surfaced in apparatus)

**Why this needs attention:** The entry documents one of the HB's
most distributionally extensive recurring formulae — well over 70
attestations across the Torah, Historical Books, Prophets, and
post-exilic literature. The cross-corpus inventory is significant
and will govern future chapter translations (every Isaian and
Jeremianic prophetic-call deployment; the Joshua-Judges-Samuel-
Kings military-encouragement deployments; the post-exilic Zech-Hag
restoration deployments). The ANE context (Mari-letters, Neo-
Assyrian *Esarhaddon-prophecies* and *Ashurbanipal-prophecies*,
Egyptian royal-encouragement texts) is documented from **Conrad**
1985 *Fear Not Warrior* (Brown Judaic Studies 75; the foundational
monograph) and **Nissinen** 2003 *Prophets and Prophecy in the
Ancient Near East* (SBL Writings from the Ancient World 12). The
entry is large but is intended as the project's reference treatment
of the formula. Reviewer should confirm the cross-corpus inventory
is accurate and that the Conrad / Nissinen ANE framing is right-
sized — not under-emphasizing the genre-comparativist work, not
over-claiming on the basis of it.

**Recommendation:** Ship.

### `anokhi-elohei-X-avikha-ancestral-god-formula`

**Source:** *anokhi Elohei Avraham avikha* (Gen 26:24)

**WoH choice:** *I am the Elohim of Avraham your father* (WoH
preserves *Elohim* in transliteration per `elohim-as-translation`;
the Alt 1929 reconstruction surfaced in apparatus)

**Why this needs attention:** The entry takes a position on one of
the foundational reconstructions in 20th-century OT scholarship —
**Albrecht Alt**'s 1929 *Der Gott der Väter* (*The God of the
Fathers*) reconstruction of the patriarchal-period ancestral-god
religion. The Alt reconstruction has been substantially refined
(Cross 1973 *Canaanite Myth and Hebrew Epic* ch. 1; Albright; de
Pury 1992) and partly critiqued (Van Seters 1975 *Abraham in
History and Tradition*; Thompson 1974 *The Historicity of the
Patriarchal Narratives*); the modern critical consensus (Miller
2000 *The Religion of Ancient Israel*; Smith 1990/2002 *The Early
History of God*) holds the broad framework plausible while
contesting the specific 2nd-millennium-BCE historicity. The entry
presents the Alt framework as foundational without committing the
project to its 2nd-millennium historicity; the Exod 3:6 burning-
bush *Anokhi Elohei avikha Elohei Avraham Elohei Yitzchaq v-Elohei
Ya'aqov* deployment is noted as the keystone cross-corpus link.
Reviewer should confirm the Alt-and-subsequent-refinement framing
is right and that the historicity-contested status is properly
flagged.

**Recommendation:** Ship.

### `metzacheq-conjugal-vs-contested-disambiguation`

**Source:** *v-hineh Yitzchaq m-tzacheq et Rivkah ishto* (Gen 26:8)

**WoH choice:** *Yitzchaq was sporting with Rivkah his wife* (ASV-
baseline; the conjugal sense surfaced in apparatus; the wordplay-
with-Yitzchaq's-name preserved)

**Why this needs attention:** The entry is `claim_type=direct`, but
the Editor's call on the claim_type deserves a second look. The
polysemy of piel *tz-ch-q* is direct lexical fact (HALOT, BDB, the
standard commentaries). The cross-corpus disambiguation framework
(the six-or-seven semantic registers across the Gen 17-26 + Exod 32
+ Judg 16 attestations) is documentary-derivable from the lexical
search. The Gen 26:8 conjugal sense itself is modern-critical-
consensus (Sarna, Alter, Westermann, NJPS, NRSV, NIV-2011, CSB).
The arguable case for `claim_type=inferred` would rest on the
*disambiguation-framework* being a scholarly synthesis rather than
a lexical-explicit fact — i.e., the project's choice to organize
the polysemy into named registers and to frame the Gen 26:8 / Gen
21:9 attestations as "structurally distinct" is itself a critical-
synthesis move. The Editor judged this to fall on the *direct* side
because the framework is derivable from the lexica plus the
standard commentaries; the polysemy and the disambiguation are not
the project's invention. Reviewer's call.

**Recommendation:** Ship as `direct`; downgrade to `inferred` if
Reviewer prefers the more cautious framing.

### `esek-sitnah-rechovot-well-name-triad`

**Source:** *Esek*, *Sitnah*, *Rechovot* (Gen 26:20-22)

**WoH choice:** Transliterated names per WoH compound-place-name
convention; the in-text etiologies translated literally so the
*contention → opposition → room* progression is visible

**Why this needs attention:** The entry **flags-but-does-not-
conflate** the *s-t-n* root-connection between *Sitnah* (the place-
name at Gen 26:21) and *ha-satan* (the adversarial-figure at Job
1-2, Zech 3, 1 Chr 21:1). The two attestations share the
philological root but inhabit distinct semantic registers — the
place-name is in the *legal-or-territorial-contention* register;
*ha-satan* is in the *divine-council-prosecutor* register. The
not-conflated treatment follows **Sarna** *JPS Genesis* 1989 p. 186
and **Westermann** *Genesis 12-36* 1985 p. 426. The cross-reference
to the *ha-satan* lexeme-cluster (to be governed by the future Job
1-2 and Zech 3 entries) is flagged in the rationale for
forward-compatibility. Reviewer should confirm the not-conflated
treatment is right and that the cross-reference forward-link is
helpful rather than confusing.

**Recommendation:** Ship.

### `morat-ruach-bitterness-of-spirit-esau-hittite-wives`

**Source:** *va-tihyeinah morat ruach l-Yitzchaq u-l-Rivkah* (Gen
26:35; with Gen 26:34 wife-list)

**WoH choice:** *a bitterness of spirit to Yitzchaq and to Rivkah*
(KJV-baseline fixed-phrase preservation)

**Why this needs attention:** The entry bundles three editorial
issues that the Translator flagged separately: (a) the *Chitti* /
Hittite identification (the entry **does not** apply the
`bnei-het-hittites-canaanite-anatolian-question` entry here; the
Editor's call is that the Gen 23 Hebron-Hittite-trading context and
the Gen 26:34 Esav-Hittite-wife context are distinct attestations
of the *Chittim*-question and should be flagged separately —
Reviewer may judge differently); (b) the wife-list inconsistency
across Gen 26:34 / 28:9 / 36:2-3 (documented in the rationale as a
source-critical problem; the rabbinic-traditional harmonization and
the modern critical multi-source attribution are both flagged);
(c) the *morat ruach* setup for Gen 27 (the dialogue-quotation
back-link at Gen 27:46). The entry's claim is `direct` because each
of the three issues is documentary; the bundling itself is the
Editor's organizational decision. Reviewer should confirm the
bundling is right (one entry vs. three smaller entries) and the
non-application of `bnei-het-hittites-canaanite-anatolian-question`
here is the right call.

**Recommendation:** Ship.
