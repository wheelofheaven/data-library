## Central-vs-overlay architectural decisions

The ratified split (chapter-singular / near-singular → overlay; cross-corpus reach → central) was applied as follows. The chapter's heavy moral-theological / commercial-vocabulary cluster prompted careful central-vs-overlay deliberation on each lemma.
