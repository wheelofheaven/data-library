# Chapter 49 Editor Report

Genesis 49 is the chapter where the patriarchal narrative receives its
tribal-political-eschatological framing: the institutional twelve-tribes
structure articulated as a poetic oracle; the Yehudah-Davidic-Messianic
line introduced via the *shevet-Shiloh* crux at v 10; the Yosef-tribal
blessing receiving the longest divine-title cluster in the corpus
(*avir Ya'aqov* + *ro'eh even Yisra'el* at v 24; *El* + *Shaddai* +
the cosmic three-tier blessing at v 25); the chapter's unique narrator-
and-YHWH-name prayer-interjection at v 18; the *ne'esaf el-ammav*
death-formula bracketing the burial-instruction at vv 29 + 33;
the Le'ah-at-Makhpelah / Rachel-on-the-road matriarchal-burial
institutional-distinction at v 31. The chapter contains TWO of the
corpus's most-debated single verses (v 10 the *Shiloh* crux; v 22 the
*ben porat Yosef* crux), the only chapter-occurrence of YHWH in Gen 49,
and the most concentrated reception-history weight in the entire Yosef
cycle.

The chapter required the project's most-substantial single-chapter
glossary investment: **twelve new central entries** plus **fourteen
central appliesTo extensions** plus **substantial overlay investment**:
**twenty-eight new overlay entries** plus **one new bidirectional
central-to-central sibling-pair** (the project's **FIFTH** central-to-
central pair: the death-formula register-pair) plus **one new
bidirectional central-to-central sibling-pair within the new entries**
(the chapter's **SEVENTH** central-to-central pair: the v 24 divine-
titles cluster). All 34 `editorial_questions[]` were resolved without
escalation. No `claim_type=speculative` entries are advanced.

The translator's draft was strong throughout — particularly at the
high-lens-risk concentrations (v 10 *Shiloh*; v 18 *YHWH*-interjection;
vv 22-26 Yosef-oracle divine-titles cluster), where the translator's
strict named-scholarship-discipline matched the editor's standard. The
editorial pass resolved every flagged item via glossary entry, appliesTo
extension, or commentary cross-reference. New entries sit at `direct`
(36) or `inferred` (4 — the chapter's literary-observation entries on
the cycle-wide name-shift pattern, the doubled-summons strophic-formula,
the *Shaddai-shadayim* paronomasia per Cross-Freedman, and the closing
editorial-coda framing curses-as-blessings). The translation surface
stays ASV-baseline 1901 with WoH proper-name transliteration; no WoH-
distinctive synthesis appears on the surface or in any rationale.
