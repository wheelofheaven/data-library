## The lens-relevant moment

**v 10** — Lot's sees the Jordan plain "like the garden of YHWH, like the land of Mitsrayim, before YHWH destroyed Sodom and Amorah." The lexical back-reference *gan-YHWH* to the Eden garden of Gen 2-3 frames the Sodom plain as a second Eden, which then makes its destruction in ch 19 an Eden-loss type-scene. Editor's commentary stays in mainstream scholarly register (Westermann, Sarna, Hamilton, Alter, von Rad all note the back-reference); the WoH posture is held to one sentence in the entry rationale.
