## Why `awaiting-human` despite the clean sweep

The Reviewer landed on `awaiting-human` for **three project-level posture affirmations** — these aren't translation-direction choices (the English is verified clean across all 21 verses), they're explicit ratifications of how the WoH corpus handles three of the most theologically loaded sites in the Hebrew Bible.

### 1. v 6 — Pauline-Reformation reception posture

**The verse:** *v-he'emin b-YHWH va-yachshebeha lo tzedakah* — "And he believed in YHWH; and he reckoned it to him for righteousness." (ASV rendering retained.)

**Why this needs explicit posture ratification:** One of the **three most-quoted Hebrew Bible verses in the entire Pauline corpus** and the foundational proof-text of the Reformation *sola fide* doctrine (Luther vs. Trent; Rom 4:3, 4:9, 4:22; Gal 3:6; the opposite-use at James 2:23).

**The Editor's posture (Reviewer-approved as technically clean):**
- The English carries no forensic-imputation language
- The new glossary entry stays close to the Hebrew lexical core (*aman* hiphil + *chashav* qal + *tzedakah*)
- The Pauline reception is **noted as downstream Greek-and-Latin**, NOT preempted in the entry
- New Perspective scholarship (Sanders 1977, Dunn 1988, Wright 2013) cited alongside traditional Reformation reading evenhandedly
- The James 2:23 opposite-use is explicitly acknowledged

**What you're ratifying:** That the WoH corpus handles the v 6 verse and its Pauline-Reformation reception in this **lens-in-the-apparatus** posture across all downstream material (wiki entries, long-form articles, future Romans translation if/when).

### 2. v 17 — the *tanur-ashan v-lappid-esh* theophany lens-posture

**The verse:** "And it came to pass that when the sun went down and it was dark, behold, a smoking furnace and a flaming torch that passed between these pieces."

**Why this needs explicit posture ratification:** The most concretely described theophany in the entire Abraham cycle, and arguably one of the most physically-rendered theophanies in the Hebrew Bible. Smoking furnace (*tanur ashan*) + flaming torch (*lappid esh*), passing between the split animals as the sole party to a unilaterally-cut covenant. This is the **highest-lens-leverage verse in the Abraham cycle to date.**

**The Editor's posture (Reviewer-affirmed as genuine compliance, not formal):**
- The translated English carries no WoH-distinctive vocabulary
- No craft, no propulsion, no engineering language intrudes
- The entry's rationale stays in mainstream Westermann/von Rad/Wenham/Sarna/Hamilton register
- Fire-and-smoke theophany pattern cross-references (Exod 19:18, 13:21, Isa 6:4, Ezek 1) presented as canonical scholarly observation
- The unilateral-divine-passage reading is the consensus modern critical reading

**What you're ratifying:** That the WoH translation of Gen 15:17 reads as defensible scholarly translation, with the WoH lens engaging the theophany **only in commentary apparatus**, not in the translated text. The lens-relevant reading (whatever the project takes the smoking-furnace-and-flaming-torch to be) lives in the wiki, not in the Hebrew.

### 3. vv 13-16 — the 400 / 430 chronology dispute

**The text:** YHWH's prophecy that Abram's seed will be sojourners in a foreign land for **400 years**, then return in the fourth generation. Exod 12:40-41 has **430 years** for the same period; LXX makes both passages 430; Acts 7:6 and Gal 3:17 follow the LXX 430.

**Why this needs explicit posture ratification:** Touches the **Exodus chronology** and downstream historicity questions (when was Abraham? when was the Exodus? was the sojourn 400 or 430 years?). Hoffmeier 1997 *Israel in Egypt* and Kitchen 2003 *On the Reliability of the Old Testament* engage; Sarna's JPS Exodus excursus surveys.

**The Editor's posture (Reviewer-approved as appropriate):**
- The 400/430 dispute is documented in the glossary entry
- NT reception (Acts 7:6, Gal 3:17 following LXX) is cited
- **No definitive position is taken**
- The chronology is left as-is in the translation (400 years per MT)

**What you're ratifying:** That the WoH Genesis translation **does not commit to a chronology** at this layer — neither historicizing the 400 years against Egyptian dynasty dates nor harmonizing with the Exodus 430. Future tooling (timeline integration, downstream wiki) can engage the question separately.
