## The two load-bearing entries

### `me-et-yhwh-min-ha-shamayim-two-powers-debate` (v 24)

The doubled-YHWH phrasing: *va-YHWH himtir al-Sodom v-al-Amorah gophrit va-esh **me-et YHWH min ha-shamayim*** — "YHWH rained on Sodom and Amorah brimstone and fire from YHWH from heaven." The Abraham cycle's most theologically charged glossary addition since the shloshah-anashim entry.

**Reviewer's verification:** *"Rabbinic (b. Sanhedrin 38b Rav Chisda exchange) and Christian (Justin Dial. 56-60 kyrios para kyriou) reception accurately reported; Segal/Boyarin/Hurtado/Bauckham scholarship correctly framed as documenting receptional history, not endorsing two-powers as original meaning; explicit Critical Framing and Lens-Stays-In-Apparatus paragraphs; translation editorially neutral."*

### `qitor-ha-kivshan-furnace-smoke-comparison` (v 28)

Abraham sees Sodom from afar — *qitor ha-aretz ke-qitor ha-kivshan* — "the smoke of the land went up like the smoke of the furnace." The most physically-concrete destruction-image in the Hebrew Bible.

**Reviewer's verification:** *"Lexical rarity analysis correct; Sodom-Sinai cross-corpus pairing via kivshan-simile editorially significant; Bunch et al. 2021* Scientific Reports *Tall el-Hammam hypothesis cited as published peer-reviewed but with explicit disclosure of contested status and multiple subsequent geoarchaeological responses contesting shocked-quartz identification, dating, and mechanism. Consensus is NOT overstated."*

The downstream WoH synthesis on the destruction-as-physical-event (impact / meteor / volcanic / archaeological) lives in the wiki, not in the glossary entry.

## The reception-sensitive entries

### `sodom-as-sodomy-reception-vs-inhospitality` (vv 4-11)

Both readings parallel, neither preempted:
- **Christian patristic + medieval** (Augustine, Aquinas; the *sodomite* word-derivation)
- **Modern critical** (Sarna, Brueggemann, Letellier 1995, Tornkvist 1998, Carden 2004 *Sodomy: A History of a Christian Biblical Myth*, Loader 1990) — inhospitality-and-violence reading. **Ezek 16:48-50** cited (Sodom's sin enumerated as pride / plenty / ease / not aiding the poor — NOT sexual).
- Judg 19 verbatim parallel (Gibeah outrage) noted.

### `lot-offers-daughters-modern-ethical-reading` (v 8)

Trible 1984 *Texts of Terror*, Bach 1997/1999, Fewell & Gunn 1993 *Gender, Power, and Promise*, Exum *Plotted, Shot, Painted* cited. Narrator's silence noted; modern feminist-critical reading framed as mainstream scholarly response.

### `moab-ammon-etiology-incest` (vv 30-38)

Folk-etymologies (Moab from *me-av*, Ben-Ammi from *ben ammi*) + Deut 23:4-5 exclusion + Ruth-Boaz Moabite-ancestry tension (David's Moabite great-grandmother) + Bauer 1999 *Reading Lot's Daughters* + Mesha Stele. Polemic-against-neighbors framing in mainstream-scholarly register.
