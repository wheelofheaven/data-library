# Chapter 24 Editor Report

The longest single narrative chapter in the Abraham cycle (67 verses)
and the chapter that closes the Abraham cycle proper: the servant's
bride-quest to Aram-Naharaim, the well-side recognition-scene with
Rivkah, the betrothal negotiation with Lavan and Bethuel, and Rivkah's
arrival in Sarah's tent. Glossary-density is concentrated in vv 1-27;
the long recitation-block at vv 34-49 mechanically re-uses the lexical
decisions made in the opening and is left commentary-light by Editor's
judgment.

The chapter is **low-to-mid lens-leverage**. Three points carry lens
weight (the *Elohei ha-shamayim* late-period-formula question; the
*chesed v-emet* virtue-pair as foundational HB ethical vocabulary; the
*l-alfei revavah* matriarchal-blessing as the structural counterpart
to the patriarchal-seed-comparison-triad), but at each point the
modern critical scholarly position is the WoH default and the
project-distinctive synthesis is reserved for the wiki.

**Cycle-closing significance.** This chapter closes the Abraham cycle
proper. The cycle's structural triptych is Gen 22 (the *aqedah* — the
binding of the seed-child), Gen 23 (the death of Sarah — the burial of
the matriarch), Gen 24 (the bride-quest — the arrival of the next
matriarch). Avraham does not appear as an actor again: Gen 25:1-11
narrates his second marriage and his death in summary. The Reviewer
should note that this chapter's editorial-review constitutes the final
review-point of the Abraham cycle's principal narrative material.

## Items the Reviewer should attend to

### `yad-tachat-yarekh-oath-gesture` — the ANE oath-by-the-thigh

**Source:** שִׂים־נָא יָדְךָ תַּחַת יְרֵכִי (*sim-na yadkha tachat
yereki*)
**Refs:** GEN-WOH-24:2, 24:9; cross-applies to GEN-47:29
**WoH choice:** *Put, I pray you, your hand under my thigh* (ASV
literal; Hebrew euphemism preserved in translation).
**Issue:** The gesture is widely read in modern scholarship as a
euphemism for touching the genital area — the seat of generative
power, the source of the patriarchal seed-line whose continuation the
oath is meant to secure. The reading is the modern critical consensus
(Sarna *JPS* p. 163; Westermann *Genesis 12-36* p. 384; Wenham *WBC*
p. 141; Hamilton *NICOT* pp. 139-140; Alter p. 117; Speiser *AB* p.
178). Two specialized treatments are pivotal: **Malul 1985** (*VT* 35,
'More on *Pahad Yitzhaq* and the Oath by the Thigh') develops the
genital-touch reading and proposes the parallel to the *pachad
Yitzchaq* (*Thigh of Yitzchaq*) at Gen 31:42, 53; **Hill 1967** (*JBL*
86, 'New Light on the Oath of Genesis 24:2') treats the gesture in its
formal-juridical register. Rashi at Gen 24:2 cites *Pirqei de-Rabbi
Eliezer* 16 and reads the gesture as touching the circumcised member,
connecting the oath to the *brit milah* covenantal-organ.
**ANE-comparative gap:** the oath-on-fertility-symbol pattern is
widespread in the Mesopotamian, Hittite, Ugaritic, and Egyptian
corpora — oaths by the cult-statue's genitals, by the king's sceptre,
by the bull's testicles — but the specific gesture of *touching
another person's genital area to swear an oath* is rare in extant ANE
texts. The two HB occurrences (Gen 24:2/9 and Gen 47:29) are the
principal documentary attestation. The Reviewer should confirm the
ANE-comparative gap framing and the genital-touch reading are
appropriately handled in the apparatus.
**Recommendation:** ship as-is. The Hebrew euphemism is preserved in
the translation; the modern critical genital-touch reading is
surfaced in the commentary and rationale; the *Gen 47:29 / pachad
Yitzchaq* cross-references are documented.

### `yhwh-elohei-ha-shamayim-divine-name` — the late-period-formula question

**Source:** יְהוָה אֱלֹהֵי הַשָּׁמַיִם וֵאלֹהֵי הָאָרֶץ (v 3); יְהוָה
אֱלֹהֵי הַשָּׁמַיִם (v 7)
**Refs:** GEN-WOH-24:3, 24:7
**WoH choice:** *YHWH, the Elohim of the heavens and the Elohim of
the earth* (v 3); *YHWH, the Elohim of the heavens* (v 7). Article-
bearing construct per established Elohim-policy.
**Issue:** First HB occurrence of *Elohei ha-shamayim*. Cross-corpus
concentration is striking: Jonah 1:9, Ezra 1:2, 5:11-12, 6:9-10,
7:12-23, Neh 1:4-5, 2:4-20, Dan 2:18-44, Ps 136:26 — Persian-period
Aramaic-and-Hebrew literature. **Two readings stand in modern critical
scholarship.** **(a) Late-composition:** Westermann (*Genesis 12-36*
pp. 384-385) and Hamilton (*NICOT* pp. 138-139) read the Gen 24
occurrence as evidence the chapter was composed or finalized in the
Persian period; the formula is a stock-phrase available to Persian-
period writers for naming YHWH in international-political contexts
(the formula is used by Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes in their decrees
as preserved in Ezra). **(b) Diplomatic-context:** Sarna (*JPS* p.
164) reads the Gen 24 occurrence as a context-sensitive idiom:
Avraham is commissioning a foreign-land journey, and the formula
establishes YHWH's universal sovereignty in cosmographic-binitarian
terms a foreign audience would recognize. Wenham (*WBC* p. 140) leans
toward Sarna. The two readings are not exclusive; the diplomatic-
context register may itself be a Persian-period feature. The Reviewer
should attend to the framing: the entry presents both readings
without preempting either, and the WoH translation preserves the
article-bearing construct without taking a dating-position.
**Recommendation:** ship as-is. Both readings are documented; the
dating question is left open in the apparatus.

### `chesed-v-emet-steadfast-love-and-truth` — the foundational HB virtue-pair

**Source:** חֶסֶד וֶאֱמֶת (*chesed v-emet*)
**Refs:** GEN-WOH-24:27, 24:49 (initial scope); cross-corpus reach
documented to 20+ verses (Gen 32:11, 47:29; Exod 34:6; Josh 2:14; 2
Sam 2:6, 15:20; Ps 25, 40, 57, 61, 85, 86, 89, 138; Prov 3, 14, 16,
20; Hos 4:1; Mic 7:20).
**WoH choice:** *chesed and truth* (chesed preserved untranslated per
established WoH lexeme-preservation policy; emet rendered as
*truth*).
**Issue:** The foundational HB virtue-pair, with no single English
equivalent for *chesed*. **Standard scholarly literature:** Glueck
1927 *Das Wort Hesed* (ET *Hesed in the Bible*, 1967) — the
foundational covenantal reading; Sakenfeld 1978 *The Meaning of Hesed
in the Hebrew Bible* (HSM 17) — refines Glueck: chesed as the action
of a more-powerful party on behalf of a less-powerful party in an
established relationship, where the more-powerful could have
declined; the modern critical consensus; Clark 1993 *The Word Hesed in
the Hebrew Bible* (JSOTSup 157) — corpus-comprehensive statistical
analysis. The Exod 34:6 keystone (the YHWH self-revelation at Sinai —
*rav-chesed v-emet*) gives the pair canonical-formula status; the
chapter's prayer (Gen 24:27) thus addresses YHWH in the very terms
YHWH will use to self-reveal at Sinai. The structural-thematic
anticipation is editorially deliberate. **The translation-choice
discussion:** the standard English renderings — KJV *lovingkindness*
(Coverdale's neologism), ASV *mercy*, NRSV *steadfast love*, NJPS
*kindness* — all flatten the lexeme. The WoH lexeme-preservation
matches the project's standing policy on *nefesh*, *Elohim*, *YHWH*,
*Torah*. The Reviewer should confirm the lexeme-preservation framing
and the Sakenfeld/Glueck/Clark scholarly tradition framing are
appropriate.
**Recommendation:** ship as-is. The cross-corpus reach is documented;
the *appliesTo* will be expanded as those passages are translated.

### `l-alfei-revavah-matriarchal-fertility-blessing` — the matriarchal-canonical formula

**Source:** אֲחֹתֵנוּ אַתְּ הֲיִי לְאַלְפֵי רְבָבָה וְיִירַשׁ זַרְעֵךְ
אֵת שַׁעַר שֹׂנְאָיו
**Refs:** GEN-WOH-24:60 (unique HB occurrence in this exact form)
**WoH choice:** *Our sister, may you become thousands of ten
thousands, and may your seed possess the gate of those who hate them*
(literal ASV register, with the obsolete *be thou the mother of*
modernized to *may you become*).
**Issue:** The verse contains an explicit verbal cross-reference to
Gen 22:17 — *v-yiyrash zar'akha et sha'ar oyvav* — establishing a
structural-literary inclusio across the Abraham cycle (the bride enters
the patriarchal family under a blessing that mirrors the patriarch's
own). The cross-reference is editorially deliberate; the modern
critical consensus (Sarna *JPS* p. 169; Westermann p. 387; Alter p.
122; Hamilton pp. 148-149) reads the verse as such. The formula is
the **canonical matriarchal counterpart to the patriarchal-seed-
comparison-triad** (dust at Gen 13:16, stars at 15:5, sand at 22:17 —
see the existing *patriarchal-seed-comparison-triad* entry). The
masculine-patriarchal formulae use cosmographic-natural similes; the
matriarchal formula uses a numerical-arithmetic expression (*alfei
revavah* — *thousands of ten-thousands*). **Trible 1978** (*God and
the Rhetoric of Sexuality*) reads the lexical distinction as part of
the HB's gendered-blessing rhetoric. **Ruth 4:11-12** is the canonical
confirmation: the elders and people at the gate of Bethlehem bless
Ruth with the explicit invocation of the matriarchs (Rachel, Le'ah)
and extend the formula to Tamar — completing the four-matriarch +
Tamar tableau that underwrites the Davidic genealogy. The Reviewer
should confirm the structural-counterpart-to-patriarchal-triad framing
and the Ruth 4:11-12 canonical-recurrence framing are appropriately
handled.
**Recommendation:** ship as-is. The Gen 22:17 cross-reference is
documented; the *patriarchal-seed-comparison-triad* entry is NOT
extended to Gen 24:60 (the lexical register differs); the Ruth
4:11-12 recurrence is documented in the rationale for forward-
reference.

### `la-suach-ba-sadeh-hapax` — the six-reading hapax

**Source:** לָשׂוּחַ בַּשָּׂדֶה (*la-suach ba-sadeh*)
**Refs:** GEN-WOH-24:63 (HB hapax — only canonical occurrence of the
verb-form)
**WoH choice:** *to meditate in the field* — ASV/Vulgate default.
**Issue:** The most-discussed lexical crux in the chapter. **Six
readings:** (1) 'meditate' — Vulgate, ASV/KJV/ESV, Ibn Ezra; (2)
'converse / chatter' — LXX *adoleschēsai*; (3) 'pray' — Targum
Onkelos, Rashi, **b. Berakhot 26b** (the canonical rabbinic-liturgical
reading: Yitzchaq instituted the afternoon *Minchah*); (4) 'commune /
converse' — Speiser, some modern; (5) 'complain / lament' — minority
modern philological; (6) 'wander / walk about' — NRSV, modern
philological from *sh-w-ch*. The **b. Berakhot 26b** reading is
liturgically-canonical in Rabbinic Judaism: the three-times-daily
prayer-cycle's patriarchal-etiology rests on this verse (Avraham
instituting *Shacharit* from Gen 19:27; Yitzchaq the *Minchah* from
this verse; Ya'aqov the *Ma'ariv* from Gen 28:11). The WoH choice of
'meditate' is editorially-conservative — neutral on the meditate-vs.-
pray-vs.-wander question — and does not preempt the rabbinic-canonical
reading. The apparatus carries the six-reading documentation. The
Reviewer should confirm the framing and may want to consider whether a
'pray' default (following the rabbinic-canonical reading) would be
preferable. The Editor's recommendation is to retain 'meditate' as the
conservative default; the apparatus does the lifting on the rabbinic
alternative.
**Recommendation:** ship as-is unless the Reviewer prefers a 'pray'
default. The 'meditate' choice matches ASV/Vulgate and is
editorially-conservative on a HB hapax; the rabbinic-canonical reading
is preserved in the apparatus.
