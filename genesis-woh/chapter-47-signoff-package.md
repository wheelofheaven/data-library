# Genesis 47 — sign-off package

**Status:** reviewer-approved (clean approve sweep; sign-off is formality)
**Chapter:** 47 — **The settlement, the famine-policy, and the burial-oath.** Five brothers presented to Pharaoh; the *eretz Ra'meses* land-grant; Ya'aqov blesses Pharaoh (twice — the subordinate blesses the sovereign) and delivers his famous pilgrimage-speech: *yemei shenei megurai sheloshim u-me'at shanah me'at ve-ra'im hayu yemei shenei chayyai* "the days of the years of my pilgrimage are 130; few and evil have been the days of the years of my life." **The famine policy** (vv13-26): Yosef sells grain for silver, then for livestock, then for land, then for bodies; the *chimmesh* fifth-tax (proposed at Gen 41:34) is inaugurated as a *choq* "statute" *ad ha-yom ha-zeh*; the priests' land alone exempt; populations relocated. Yisra'el settles, *va-ye'achazu vah va-yifru va-yirbu me'od* (the *achuzzah* inclusio + *peru u-rvu* creation-blessing fulfilment). Seventeen years in Mitsrayim; total life 147. The burial-oath: *sim na yadekha tachat yerekhi... al-na tiqberenni be-Mitzrayim* — and Yisra'el bows upon the bed's head (MT) / head of the staff (LXX, Heb 11:21).
