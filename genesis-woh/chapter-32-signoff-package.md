## The chapter's signature wins

**`peniel-panim-el-panim-divine-face-encounter`** — Jacob's *panim el panim* with Elohim + *va-tinatzel nafshi* (survived-the-theophany). Foundational HB cross-corpus chain: Exod 33:11 (Moses), Exod 33:20 (no one shall see and live), Deut 5:4 (Sinai), Deut 34:10 (no prophet since Moses), Judg 6:22 (Gideon). The survived-theophany trope.

**`yisrael-name-aetiology-sarah-im-elohim`** — Five positions documented: Cross's *El strives* default; in-text *one-who-strives-with-El*; Ibn Ezra's *El is upright*; Albright's *El rules*; Speiser's rejected Ugaritic *yšr*. Translation preserves the in-text etymology while the apparatus documents the modern-critical default. Hos 12:4-5 reception correctly identified.

**`gid-ha-nasheh-sciatic-nerve-dietary-rule`** — Translation preserves the *nasheh* hapax via "sinew of the thigh-vein" (correctly resisting over-translation as "sciatic nerve"). Categorical: only HB dietary-rule with a narrative aetiology. Later codification in Mishnah Chullin 7 + b. Chullin 89b-103b.
