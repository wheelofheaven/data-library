## Other notable additions

### `tiqun-soferim-yhwh-stood-before-abraham` (v 22)

One of the 18 canonical Masoretic *tiqune soferim* (scribal-emendation tradition). The original *v-YHWH odenu omed lifnei Avraham* ("YHWH still stood before Abraham") was emended for reverence to *v-Avraham odenu omed lifnei YHWH*. Both McCarthy 1981 *The Tiqqune Sopherim* (conservative-traditional reading: actual scribal emendations) and Tov 2012 (retrospective interpretive flag) presented in parallel. Full Masorah citations (b. Megillah 25b, Bereshit Rabbah 49:7, Tanchuma Yashan Beshallach 16, Mekhilta de-Rabbi Yishmael Shirta 6). MT preserved per established WoH textual-conservative practice.

### `puncta-extraordinaria-v9-elav` (v 9)

One of the 15 canonical *puncta extraordinaria* / *nequdot*. The four supralinear dots over אֵׄלָׄיׄוׄ at "they said to him, where is Sarah your wife?" Rabbinic interpretation (b. Bava Metzia 87a, Bereshit Rabbah 48:15) + modern scholarly interpretation (Tov 2012, McCarthy 1981, Yeivin 1980 *Tiberian Masorah*) presented in parallel.

### `divine-descent-to-investigate-pattern` (vv 20-21)

The *erdah-na v-er'eh* formula. Cross-corpus pattern: Gen 11:5-7 (Babel), Exod 3:8 (Egypt), Exod 19 (Sinai), Mic 1:3, Isa 64:1. Reviewer-verified: "The entry terminates at Hamori's spatial-anthropomorphism reading and explicitly disclaims further interpretive move. The lens-relevant WoH downstream synthesis stays out of the entry."
