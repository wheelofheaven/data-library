### `sodom-as-sodomy-reception-vs-inhospitality` (v 5)

**Source:** הוֹצִיאֵם אֵלֵינוּ וְנֵדְעָה אֹתָם
**WoH choice:** preserves the literal *that we may know them*
(ASV-baseline) and documents both the patristic-medieval *sodomy*
reading and the modern critical *gang-rape-violence and inhospitality*
reading in parallel without preempting either.

**Why careful framing matters:** This is the reception-history
flashpoint of the entire Sodom narrative and the highest-stakes
single verse in the chapter on the cultural-historical axis. The
entry documents:

- The patristic-medieval *sodomia* category (Augustine *De Civitate
  Dei* XVI.30; Aquinas *Summa Theologiae* II-II q. 154 a. 11; the
  medieval penitentials; the etymological pathway of *sodomy* into
  the European languages from the Latin place-name).
- The modern critical reading (Sarna 1989; Brueggemann 1982;
  Westermann 1985; Letellier 1995; Loader 1990; Tornkvist 1998;
  Carden 2004 *Sodomy: A History of a Christian Biblical Myth*).
- Four converging arguments for the inhospitality reading: Ezek
  16:48-50 enumeration of Sodom's sin as pride / surfeit / ease /
  failure to aid the poor (the most decisive HB-internal evidence);
  the wider HB Sodom-reference pattern (Isa 1:9-10, 3:9; Jer 23:14;
  Lam 4:6; Wis 19:13-17); the *za'aqah* mechanism (cross-referenced
  to the existing `zaaqah-outcry-of-the-oppressed` entry); and the
  Judg 19 Gibeah parallel-scene.

**Recommendation:** ship. The entry is at strict accuracy-above-lens
discipline. Both readings are presented in parallel; neither is
preempted in the translation or in the entry's editorial framing.
The translation preserves the literal ASV-baseline.

### `me-et-yhwh-min-ha-shamayim-two-powers-debate` (v 24)

**Source:** וַיהוָה הִמְטִיר ... מֵאֵת יְהוָה מִן־הַשָּׁמָיִם
**WoH choice:** preserves the doubled divine name literally
(*Then YHWH rained ... from YHWH out of the heavens*, ASV-baseline).

**Why Reviewer attention (HIGH lens-leverage):** This is one of the
highest-lens-leverage entries in the entire Genesis glossary to
date. The verse is the foundational text of the rabbinic *shtei
rashuyot ba-shamayim* (*two powers in heaven*) debate and of the
early Christian binitarian / Logos-exegesis tradition. The entry
documents:

- The classical rabbinic engagement at b. Sanhedrin 38b (Rav Chisda
  vs. the heretic) and related rabbinic passages (b. Sanhedrin 38a;
  b. Chagigah 14a; Mekhilta on Exod 15:3 and 20:2).
- Justin Martyr *Dialogue with Trypho* 56-60 as the foundational
  pre-Nicene Christian use of the verse, with the wider second-
  century Logos-tradition (Theophilus, Irenaeus, Tertullian).
- Modern scholarly literature: Segal *Two Powers in Heaven* (SJLA
  25) 1977; Boyarin *Border Lines* 2004 chs 3-4; Boyarin *The Jewish
  Gospels* 2012; Hurtado *One God, One Lord* 1988/1998; Bauckham
  *Jesus and the God of Israel* 2008; Hayman 1991; MacDonald 2003.

**The discipline:** the two-powers reading is presented as a
recognized minority position in both Jewish and Christian reception
history; the entry does not endorse it as the original meaning of
Gen 19:24. The lens stays in the wiki; the entry documents the
receptional history and lets the verse be read in the apparatus
without preemption.

**Recommendation:** ship, with explicit Reviewer attention to the
"Critical framing" and "The lens stays in the apparatus" paragraphs.
This is the single most reception-sensitive verse in the chapter on
the theological axis; the framing must remain receptional-historical
rather than project-distinctive. If the Reviewer judges the framing
slips toward project-distinctive synthesis at any point, flag for
rewrite.

### `qitor-ha-kivshan-furnace-smoke-comparison` (v 28)

**Source:** קִיטֹר הָאָרֶץ כְּקִיטֹר הַכִּבְשָׁן
**WoH choice:** *the smoke of the land went up as the smoke of a
furnace* (ASV-baseline).

**Why Reviewer attention (HIGH lens-leverage):** the verse is one of
the most concretely physical destruction-images in the HB and is
adjacent to the project's downstream synthesis on the Sodom-
destruction-as-physical-event question. The entry's framing is
load-bearing. The entry documents:

- The cross-corpus chain to Exod 9:8 (*piach kivshan* — the soot of
  the furnace at the sixth plague of boils) and Exod 19:18 (the
  verbatim *k-eshen ha-kivshan* — *as the smoke of the furnace* at
  the Sinai theophany). The Sodom-overthrow / Sinai-revelation
  pairing across the Pentateuch by way of the kivshan-simile is
  editorially deliberate and worth Reviewer attention.
- The ANE technological context of the *kivshan* as standard
  pottery-kiln (~900-1100°C, dense black smoke), distinguished from
  *kur* (metal-smelting-furnace, Deut 4:20 / Egypt-iron-furnace
  tradition).
- The **Tall el-Hammam Late-Bronze-Age cosmic-airburst hypothesis**
  (Bunch et al. *Scientific Reports* 11, 21425, 2021) cited as
  scientific literature within the geoarchaeology field, **not** as
  the project's distinctive synthesis. The entry explicitly notes
  the hypothesis is contested in the subsequent peer-reviewed
  responses and is one current scientific reading rather than the
  consensus.

**The discipline:** the entry stays in mainstream-scientific
register. Project-distinctive synthesis on the meteor / asteroid /
volcanic / impact-event question, and on the wider WoH cosmological
framing of the destruction, is reserved for the wiki and long-form
material downstream. The Bunch et al. 2021 citation is presented as
published peer-reviewed scientific literature in a contested field,
not as evidence for project-distinctive claims.

**Recommendation:** ship, with explicit Reviewer attention to the
"Tall el-Hammam impact hypothesis" paragraph. If the Reviewer judges
the framing of the Bunch et al. 2021 citation slips toward
endorsement or toward project-distinctive synthesis, flag for
rewrite. The current framing is "one contested scientific reading";
the contested-status disclosure is in the entry.

### `moab-ammon-etiology-incest` (vv 30-38)

**Source:** מוֹאָב ... בֶּן־עַמִּי
**WoH choice:** ASV-baseline transliterations preserved; etymology
in apparatus.

**Why careful framing matters:** The chapter's coda is one of the
canonical HB polemics-against-neighbor-peoples. The entry documents:

- The pair-etymology of Moab (*me-av* — from-father; LXX gloss at
  Gen 19:37 *ek tou patros mou*) and Ben-Ammi (*ben ammi* — son of
  my kin; in-text at Gen 19:38).
- The historical philological evidence (Mesha Stele *KAI* 181; the
  Ammonite Citadel inscription; Neo-Assyrian and Egyptian sources
  on *Mu'aba* / *Bit-Ammana*) showing the etymologies are
  **polemical-etiological, not philological-historical** — the
  Hebrew narrative encodes a polemic, the actual etymologies are
  independent.
- The cross-corpus reception: Deut 23:4-5 exclusion from the
  assembly; Ruth (the canonical working-through of the exclusion
  via David's Moabitess ancestress); 1 Kgs 11:1 (Solomon's foreign
  wives); 2 Kgs 3 (the Moab campaign); Neh 13:1-3 (post-exilic
  re-application).
- The modern feminist-critical reception (Bauer *Reading Lot's
  Daughters* 1999) of the daughter-initiative narrative,
  cross-referenced to the `lot-offers-daughters-modern-ethical-reading`
  entry at v 8 (the chapter's structural bracketing of patriarchal-
  sexual rupture).

**Recommendation:** ship. The entry presents the polemical frame
explicitly as polemical (with the Mesha-Stele independent
philological evidence as the historical-linguistic correction);
modern scholarly consensus is mainstream; the feminist-critical
reading is documented as one current scholarly approach not as
project synthesis.

### `sanverim-blindness-hapax` (v 11)

`claim_type=direct`. The hapax-form (in the *sanverim* plural-of-
effect) attestation pair at Gen 19:11 and 2 Kgs 6:18, with the
Akkadian *šunwurum* loanword etymology (HALOT; CAD Š/III; Tigay
*JPS Excursus*) and the structural-narrative parallel to the Elisha
scene at Dotan. Mainstream HB lexicography.

### `chemlat-yhwh-compassion-formula` (v 16)

`claim_type=direct`. The lexeme *chemlah* distinct from *chesed*
and *rachamim* — sparing-from-deserved-destruction. Cross-corpus
chain: 1 Sam 15:3-15 (Agag); 2 Sam 12:4 (Nathan's parable); Mal
3:17 (sparing-as-father-sparing-son); Joel 2:18; Jon 4:10-11; Ezek
9:5 (the negative formula). Pairs structurally with v 29 *zakhar*
mechanism.

### `al-tabit-acharekha-do-not-look-back` (v 17)

`claim_type=direct`. The do-not-look-back prohibition. NT recall at
Luke 17:32 (*remember Lot's wife*) documented as direct LXX-aligned
echo. Comparative ANE / cross-cultural pattern documented (Orpheus
and Eurydice; *Descent of Inanna*; Adapa myth) **as comparative-
mythological observation, not as direct literary dependence**. The
WoH lens does not extend further into project-distinctive synthesis
from this verse. Mainstream comparative-mythology framing.

### `mahapekhah-overthrowing-formula` (v 25)

`claim_type=direct`. The *hafakh* / *mahapekhah* overthrow lexeme
and its cross-corpus chain: Deut 29:22; Isa 1:7 and 13:19; Jer 49:18
and 50:40; Amos 4:11; Lam 4:6; Zeph 2:9. *K-mahapekhat Sodom va-
Amorah* becomes the HB's fixed destruction-comparison-formula.
Mainstream HB lexical-philological work.

### `netziv-melach-pillar-of-salt` (v 26)

`claim_type=direct`. The *netziv* lexeme (from *natzav*, with the
verbal echo to the *nitzavim* of Gen 18:2); the Mt. Sodom / Jabal
Usdum geological context; the continuous identification across two
millennia (Josephus *Ant.* I.11.4; Wis 10:7; medieval pilgrim
accounts); the Bronze-Age destruction archaeology (Bab edh-Dhra,
Numeira; Rast and Schaub 2003). The historical-Sodom identification
question (Bab edh-Dhra vs. Tall el-Hammam) noted as contested and
left open in the entry; the geological etiology is independent of
the archaeological identification question.
