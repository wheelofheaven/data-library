# Chapter 44 Editor Report

Genesis 44 is the moral-and-structural climax of the Yosef cycle: the
silver-cup test, the third *hishtachavah*-fulfilment, and Yehudah's
great substitution-speech that ends mid-peroration as the chapter's
literary cliffhanger (resolved at Gen 45:1 by Yosef's
*lo-yakhol-le-hit'appeq* restraint-collapse). The chapter has the
highest single-chapter glossary-load of the cycle so far: four new
central entries, eight new overlay entries, eight existing-entry
appliesTo extensions, two bidirectional sibling-cross-reference
extensions, and the project's first central-to-central sibling-link.

The translator's draft was uniformly strong; the editorial pass
resolved all 21 editorial_questions[] without escalation. No
*claim_type=speculative* entries are advanced. All new entries are
`direct` (8) or `inferred` (4), per the chapter's mainstream-scholarly
substance. The translation surface stays ASV-baseline 1901 with WoH
proper-name transliteration; no WoH-distinctive synthesis appears on
the surface or in any rationale.

## Speculative entries requiring sign-off

**None.** No `claim_type=speculative` entries are advanced in this
pass. Every new entry sits at `direct` (anchored in well-documented

## Glossary changes for review

### Central glossary (v2.43.0 → v2.44.0; semver-minor: four additions
+ three appliesTo extensions + one rationale extension)

**Added (central):**

- `nachesh-divination-cup-yosef-cycle-cross-corpus` (Gen 44:5, 15) —
  claim_type: `direct`. The *nachash* II Piel divination-verb;
  cross-corpus reach across the Deuteronomic prohibition-list (Deut
  18:10), the H-Code (Lev 19:26), the Bil'am cycle (Num 23:23, 24:1),
  the Deuteronomistic indictments (2 Kgs 17:17, 21:6 = 2 Chr 33:6),
  and Isa 2:6. MAJOR by the corpus-wide prohibited-divination keyword
  status. The Gen 3 *nachash* serpent-homophony is noted as literary
  observation only (HALOT distinguishes the two lemmas; the
  etymological relation is debated and not adjudicated). The Yosef
  dream-interpreter-vs-cup-diviner irony (contrast with `pitron-
  dream-interpretation-yosef-cycle`) is documented. ASV-baseline
  'indeed divineth' preserves the paronomastic intensifier.

- `chalilah-far-be-it-disavowal-formula` (Gen 44:7, 17) — claim_type:
  `direct`. The corpus's standard ritual-disavowal formula; cross-
  corpus saturation across Gen 18:25 (Avraham's intercession — first
  occurrence); 1 Sam 12:23; 14:45; 22:15; 24:7; 26:11; 2 Sam 20:20;
  23:17; Job 27:5 (Iyov's integrity-climax). MAJOR. The chapter-
  internal mirror v 7 ↔ v 17 (brothers ↔ Yosef) documented. KJV's
  theologically-expanded 'God forbid' noted but not adopted on the
  surface.

- `avon-iniquity-cross-corpus-keyword` (Gen 44:16) — claim_type:
  `direct`. One of the HB's three principal sin-vocabulary terms
  (with *peshah* and *chatah*) with the three-fold semantic load
  (act/guilt/consequence). MAJOR by corpus-wide reach. Cross-corpus
  saturation across Exod 20:5 + 34:7 (divine-attribute formulae);
  Lev 16:21-22 (the scapegoat); Isa 53:5-6 + 11 (the suffering-
  servant); Jer 31:34 (the New Covenant formula); Ezek 18 (the
  generational-iniquity debate); Ps 32:5; 51:4; ~75 Psalter
  occurrences. **First central-to-central sibling-link in the
  project**: bidirectional cross-reference with the existing
  `avon-mins'so` (Gen 4:13, Qayin's first *avon*) — both rationales
  cross-reference the other by id, the two entries are deliberate
  central-companions per the rare-but-precedented pattern.

- `tachat-substitution-formula` (Gen 44:33) — claim_type: `direct`.
  The corpus's principal substitution-formula, anchoring the
  literary-theological substitution-pattern. MAJOR — the moral-and-
  structural climax of the Yosef cycle. Cross-corpus reach across
  Gen 22:13 (the Aqedah's *tachat beno* — ram-for-Yitzchaq, the
  corpus's anchor substitution-deployment); Exod 21:23-25 + Lev
  24:18-20 (the lex talionis *nefesh tachat nefesh*); Num 3:12 + 41
  + 45 (the Levites *tachat* the firstborn); the literary-theological
  cluster extending to Lev 16 (the scapegoat) and Isa 53 (the
  servant). The Gen 22:13 ↔ Gen 44:33 typological-pair is documented
  as named literary scholarship (Sternberg, Alter, Brueggemann,
  Fokkelman, Sarna). **Christian-theological atonement-reading
  explicitly NOT imposed**: the typology is documented as literary-
  scholarly observation only; no pre-commitment to substitutionary-
  atonement-theology.

**Extended appliesTo (central):**

  fulfilment-arc Gen 37:7+9+10 → 42:6 → 43:26+28 → 44:14 → 50:18 as

  fulfilment-verses; creating a separate overlay would fragment the
  arc's documentation. The literary point — that the third fulfilment
  uses the prostration-variant rather than the dream-verb — is
  documented in the per-verse commentary.

- `shever-mashbir-grain-purchase-vocabulary-yosef-cycle` += GEN-WOH-44:2
  + 44:25. Scope-formalization; the entry's rationale-anchor cross-

- `sheol-underworld-realm-of-the-dead` += GEN-WOH-44:29, 44:31. Scope-
  formalization; the entry's rationale already names 44:29 + 31 as
