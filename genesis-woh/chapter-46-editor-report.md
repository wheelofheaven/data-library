# Chapter 46 Editor Report

Genesis 46 is the cycle's descent-pivot chapter and the structural
transition from patriarchal-Kena'an to the Egyptian sojourn. The
chapter contains three structurally-major literary units that
required substantial central-glossary investment: (i) the Be'er-Sheva
theophany of vv 1-4, including the doubled-name-call, the *al-tira*
fear-not formula, the *anokhi ha-El Elohei avikha* triple-compound
divine-self-disclosure, the *goy gadol* great-nation promise, and the
*anokhi immekha* descent-and-return covenant-presence formula (the
seed-of-the-Exodus); (ii) the seventy-souls genealogy of vv 8-27 as
one of Genesis's three foundational archival-document insertions
(with Gen 5 and Gen 10), including the LXX/MT seventy-five-vs-seventy
textual-critical variant; (iii) the Ya'aqov-Yosef reunion at Goshen
in vv 28-30, including the *va-yera elav* reunion-as-theophany
lexical-echo and the *amutah ha-pa'am* death-readiness arc closure.

The chapter has the highest cross-corpus-reach single-chapter
central-glossary load of the cycle so far: five new central entries
(including one bidirectional central-overlay sibling-pair), four
existing central entries' appliesTo extended, eighteen new overlay
entries (including three bidirectional overlay sibling-pair links to
existing overlays), and three existing overlay entries' appliesTo
extended.

The translator's draft was uniformly strong; the editorial pass
resolved all 28 `editorial_questions[]` without escalation. No
`claim_type=speculative` entries are advanced. All new entries are
`direct` (16) or `inferred` (3), per the chapter's mainstream-scholarly
substance. The translation surface stays ASV-baseline 1901 with WoH
proper-name transliteration; no WoH-distinctive synthesis appears on
the surface or in any rationale.

## Speculative entries requiring sign-off

**None.** No `claim_type=speculative` entries are advanced in this
pass. Every new entry sits at `direct` (anchored in well-documented
cross-corpus lexical-distribution or grammatical fact) or `inferred`
(named-scholarship literary-theological observations between attested
readings).

The chapter's three lens-risk concentrations were handled with strict
discipline:

(a) **The descent-and-return covenant-promise at v 4 (the seed of the
Exodus).** The entry `anokhi-immekha-divine-presence-and-descent-
promise-formula` documents the construction as a named-scholarship
covenantal-programmatic structure (Cassuto's 'preview of the Exodus';
Sternberg's 'narrative-prolepsis'; Westermann on the Joseph-cycle's
connection to the Exodus-tradition; Childs on the Exod 3-6 'bring up'
programmatic; von Rad's theological-architectonic). NO Christian-
theological descent-and-resurrection typology; NO Calvinistic
compatibilism; NO WoH-distinctive synthesis on divine descent /
presence / Exodus-as-engineering. The rationale stays in named-
scholarly-literary register throughout.

(b) **The seventy-souls foundational-number at v 27.** The entry
`shivim-nefesh-seventy-souls-foundational-number-LXX-variant`
documents the LXX/MT variant via Tov *Textual Criticism* (2012),
Wevers *Notes on the Greek Text of Genesis* (1993), Fitzmyer *AB Acts*
(1998); the Qumran 4QExoda evidence; the Acts 7:14 NT-tradition. The
wider seventy-typology cross-corpus distribution (the seventy elders
of Israel, the seventy years of exile, the seventy-week-prophecy, the
Ugaritic seventy sons of El) is documented WITHOUT theological-
typological adjudication. NO Sanhedrin-typology asserted; NO
70-nations-of-Gen-10 typology asserted; NO NT-elders-typology
adjudicated.

(c) **The Gen 46:34 *to'evah*-of-shepherds Hyksos-memory observation.**
The chapter-46 commentary at v 34 documents the Vergote, Redford,
Bietak, Hoffmeier Hyksos-period scholarship and the documentary-
critical reading even-handedly; no WoH ANE-archaeological synthesis;
no adjudication. The central `toevah-abomination-cross-corpus-
keyword` rationale already documents the Gen 46:34 deployment; the
appliesTo extension is scope-formalization.
