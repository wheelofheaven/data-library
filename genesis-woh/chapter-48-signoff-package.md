# Genesis 48 — sign-off package

**Status:** reviewer-approved (clean approve sweep; sign-off is formality)
**Chapter:** 48 — **Ya'aqov's deathbed adoption-blessing of Yosef's sons.** Yosef brings Menasheh and Ephraim to his sick father. Ya'aqov recites the *El Shaddai nir'ah elai be-Luz* covenant memory and articulates **the adoption-formula**: *Ephrayim u-Menasheh ki-Re'uven ve-Shim'on yihyu li* "Ephraim and Menasheh shall be mine, as Re'uven and Shim'on" — the institutional move that gives Yosef the firstborn's double-portion through two tribes instead of one. The chapter's **theological signature** at v16: *ha-mal'akh ha-go'el oti mi-kol-ra yvarekh et-ha-ne'arim* "the messenger who hath redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads" — unifying Gen 28 (the ladder), Gen 31 (the dream-messenger), Gen 32 (the wrestling-stranger), Gen 35 (El Shaddai at Luz). The **deliberate hand-crossing**: *sikkel et-yadav ki Menasheh ha-bekhor* — Ya'aqov declines Yosef's correction with *yada'ti veni yada'ti... ulam achiv ha-qaton yigdal mimmenu* "I know, my son, I know... his younger brother shall be greater than he." The liturgical-blessing formula: *yesimkha Elohim ke-Ephrayim ve-khi-Menasheh* — the source of the Jewish *Birkat ha-Banim*. The *shekhem achad* polysemy and the *be-charbi u-ve-qashti* historical-redactional crux at v22.
