## Reviewer attention items

### `firstborn-priority-reversal-irony` (v 26) — LOAD-BEARING

Laban's defense in v 26 uses the same Hebrew **bekhirah** (firstborn-daughter) that Jacob-the-younger violated in Gen 27 (taking Esau's *bekhorah*). The literary irony is foundational to the Jacob-cycle's structure: the deceiver becomes the deceived. The narrative-rhetorical pattern continues at Gen 48 (Jacob himself blessing the younger Ephraim over the elder Manasseh). Mainstream scholarship: Alter 1981 + 2004, Sternberg 1985 *Poetics of Biblical Narrative*, Sarna *JPS* p. 205, Fokkelman 1975 *Narrative Art in Genesis*.

Verify the cross-corpus chain: Gen 25:23 oracle → Gen 27 deception → Gen 29:26 reversal → Gen 48 continued-pattern.

### `well-betrothal-type-scene` (vv 1-14)

Alter 1981 *Art of Biblical Narrative* ch 3 — the canonical well-betrothal type-scene. Three HB instances:
- Gen 24 (Eliezer-Rebekah; most-elaborated)
- Gen 29 (Jacob-Rachel; most concentrated on the action)
- Exod 2:15-22 (Moses-Zipporah; most-compressed)

Pattern: future bridegroom journeys to a foreign land; arrives at a well; meets a girl drawing water; helps her with water; she runs home to announce; the family welcomes him; betrothal follows. Verify Alter framing + Sternberg 1985 supplement.

### `einayim-rakot-leah-eyes-dispute` (v 17)

Four readings preserved:
- (a) "Weak eyes" (KJV; tradition reads physical defect)
- (b) "Tender eyes" (ASV; ambiguous gentle/soft) — **WoH choice**
- (c) "Lovely eyes" (NJPS; positive — soft = pretty)
- (d) "Tearful eyes" (some modern critical — *rakot* as moist)

ASV/Tender is the editorially-conservative middle position. Sarna, Westermann, NJPS notes present the dispute.

### `four-leah-firstborn-name-etymologies-cluster` (vv 32-35)

Four Hebrew name-aetiologies — the foundational naming-pattern for the twelve tribes:
- Reuben (*re'u-ben* / *ra'ah YHWH b-onyi*) — multiple etymological readings (Hebrew + ANE comparative); uncertain
- Simeon (*Shim'on* / *shama YHWH*) — *shama* "heard" → standard
- Levi (*Levi* / *yilavve ishi elai*) — *lavah* "joined" → standard
- Judah (*Yehudah* / *odeh et-YHWH*) — *yadah* "praise" → standard

The four become the major Israelite tribes. Cross-corpus reach: each name recurs throughout the HB. Scholarly: Sarna pp. 207-208, Westermann pp. 466-470.
