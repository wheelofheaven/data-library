# Chapter 47 Editor Report

Genesis 47 is the settlement-and-famine-policy chapter: the formal
five-man audience with Pharaoh, the Goshen / Ra'meses *achuzzah*
land-grant, Ya'aqov's blessing of Pharaoh, the three-stage famine-
policy (silver → livestock → land + bodies), the priestly-exemption,
the *chimmesh* fifth-tax codification *ad ha-yom ha-zeh*, the
seventeen-year sojourn, and the oath-thigh burial-vow. Structurally
the chapter advances the cycle from arrival (Gen 46) to settlement
+ death-prep, and bridges the Yosef-cycle to the Exodus opening
(the *peru u-rvu* multiplication, the Ra'meses-toponym, the
*avadim*-vocabulary recurrence).

The chapter required substantial central-glossary investment:
**six new central entries** plus **eight central appliesTo
extensions** (including one legacy-form correction) plus **three
central rationale extensions**; **thirteen new overlay entries**
(including one bidirectional central-overlay sibling-pair) plus
**five overlay appliesTo extensions** plus **two overlay rationale
extensions**. All 23 `editorial_questions[]` were resolved without
escalation. No `claim_type=speculative` entries are advanced.

The translator's draft was uniformly strong; the editorial pass
resolved every flagged item via glossary entry, appliesTo extension,
or commentary cross-reference. New entries sit at `direct` (16) or
`inferred` (3, the named-scholarship reading-divide and literary-
observation entries). The translation surface stays ASV-baseline
1901 with WoH proper-name transliteration; no WoH-distinctive
synthesis appears on the surface or in any rationale.

## Speculative entries requiring sign-off

**None.** No `claim_type=speculative` entries are advanced in this
pass.

The chapter's three principal lens-risk concentrations were handled
with strict named-scholarly-literary register:

(a) **The Ra'meses dating-anachronism debate (v 11).** The central
`raamses-eastern-delta-store-city-toponym` documents the Per-Ramesses
identification (Tell ed-Dab'a / Qantir; Bietak excavations) and the
three named-scholarship reading-positions on the dating: (i) Kitchen
/ Hoffmeier proleptic-naming defense (the Genesis-narrative's pre-
Ramesside historical setting; the toponym as redactional-update for
post-13th-c BCE readership); (ii) Redford / Bietak-in-part post-
Ramesside redaction reading (Genesis as 13th-c BCE or later); (iii)
mixed-tradition redactional-update reading independent of broader
composition-dating. NO theological-chronological pre-resolution. NO
WoH ANE-archaeological synthesis on the New-Kingdom-Ramesside
identification. NO Exodus-dating pre-resolution.

(b) **The famine-policy reading-divide (vv 13-26).** The overlay
`famine-policy-reading-divide-yosef-administrator-vs-enslaver-gen47`
documents the three named-scholarship reading-camps (Sarna /
Hamilton / Wenham / von Rad benevolent-administrator; Brueggemann /
Levenson / Carr / Friedman / Crüsemann enslaver-of-Mitsrayim;
Westermann / Alter / Sternberg deliberately-ambiguous) without
adjudicating. NO WoH-distinctive synthesis on Yosef-as-
administrator; NO Wheel-of-Heaven proto-totalitarian-state
synthesis; NO theological adjudication. The translation surface
remains neutral; commentary documents both readings even-handedly.

(c) **The *ad ha-yom ha-zeh* deuteronomistic-aetiological formula (v
26).** The central `ad-ha-yom-ha-zeh-aetiological-formula` documents
the formula via Childs's classic JBL 1963 article and the broader
documentary-critical tradition (Noth, Cross, Friedman, Carr,
Weinfeld) plus the minimalist-revisionist tradition (Lemche, Van
Seters, Thompson). The source-critical positions (J / Dtr / P
redactional-layer attributions) are documented without adjudicating.
NO theological pre-resolution.

(d) **The two textual variants (v 21 MT/LXX-SP and v 31 MT/LXX-
Heb).** Both overlay entries (`mt-lxx-sp-he-evir-le-arim-vs-he-evid-
la-avadim-textual-variant-gen47` and `mt-lxx-mittah-mateh-bed-vs-
staff-textual-variant-gen47`) preserve the MT per the project's
Gen 22:13 MT-fidelity baseline. The LXX, Samaritan-Pentateuch, and
Heb 11:21 variants are documented per Tov, Wevers, Skinner without
adjudication. NO textual-critical pre-resolution.
