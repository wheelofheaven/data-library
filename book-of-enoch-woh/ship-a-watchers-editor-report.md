### Notes on three deferred-to-Reviewer decisions

1. **The 1 En 1:9 Jude-tense-shift (ENO-WOH-1:9).** The Aramaic prophetic-perfect *ʾāteh* is preserved as 'he comes' per the layered-witness convention. The Jude 14 aorist *ēlthen* 'he came' is documented as the citation-form. The Reviewer should confirm that the source-tense-preservation (over Jude-tense-conformity) is the intended editorial choice. **Recommendation: ship as-is** — the layered-witness convention requires source-tense-preservation; the Jude-citation-form is documented in commentary.

2. **The Asael / Azazel onomastic convention (chs 8-16).** The Aramaic *ʿAśāʾēl* is preserved where the Aramaic fragments witness; the Greek / Ge'ez assimilated form Azazel is used where the Greek Akhmim or Ge'ez is the witness. The transition between forms within a single chapter (e.g., ch 13 where Asael in v.1 may shift to Azazel in v.6 depending on witness-availability) is the layered-witness convention but produces some readability-irregularity. The Reviewer should confirm. **Recommendation: ship as-is** — the convention is documented at overlay entry `aramaic-onomastics-preservation-convention` and at central entry `asael-azazel-knowledge-transmission-1-enoch-8-canon-engagement-cross-corpus`.

3. **The 300-vs-3000-cubits stature variant (ENO-WOH-7:2).** The Aramaic 4Q201 (where reconstructable) appears to read 300 cubits; the Greek + Ge'ez read 3000 cubits. The Translator default is 3000 cubits (per the Greek + Ge'ez majority-witness). The Reviewer should confirm. **Recommendation: ship as-is with both readings documented** — the overlay entry `300-vs-3000-cubits-giant-height-variant-1-enoch-7-2-witness-variant` preserves both witness-readings.
