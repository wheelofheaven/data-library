## Summary

BCE, predating the Aramaic Book-of-the-Watchers fragments (4Q201/4Q202; c. 200-150 BCE). The Editor pass produced the foundational central glossary entries documenting (i) the Astronomical Book as a unit with its distinctive textual-witness situation (no Greek witness; Aramaic-fuller-than-Ge'ez witness-fullness-inversion); (ii) the 364-day Qumran solar calendar polemic as the calendrical-identity-keystone of the Qumran-Enochic-Jubilees corpus; (iii) Uriel as the cosmic-administrative-angel over the luminaries; (iv) the twelve-gates and four-winds cosmography; (v) the north-as-dwelling-of-the-Most-High statement extending the 6-tradition cosmic-mountain cluster to a 7-tradition cluster; (vi) the cosmic-disorder eschatological pericope of ch 80 as one of the foundational Second-Temple-period cosmic-signs texts; (vii) the heavenly-tablets motif at ch 81; (viii) the Enoch-final-assumption with the one-year-instruction-period framework.

## Unresolved editorial questions

All 51 editorial questions across chs 72-82 have been resolved — either folded into the new central or overlay glossary entries (with `appliesTo` pointing at the relevant refIds), addressed in per-verse `commentary`, or both. No editorial question required deferral or escalation.

The principal scholarly-cruxes flagged and resolved:

### `ENO-WOH-72:7-31` — the 18-vs-22-part day-ratio dual-system

**Issue:** The chapter narrates the sun's annual circuit with day-and-night ratios stated using two parallel systems (22-part at the equinoxes-and-mid-seasons; 18-part at the solstice-extremes). The source-internal inconsistency is one of the most-discussed astronomical-textual-cruxes of the Astronomical Book.

**Resolution:** Created overlay entry `18-vs-22-part-day-ratio-scholarly-crux-1-enoch-72-7-no-harmonization-convention`. The WoH translation preserves the source-internal inconsistency verbatim without harmonization, per the foundational Neugebauer 1981 scholarly approach. The dual-system situation is the original-source-text feature; multiple proposed harmonization-strategies are documented in the overlay entry rationale without adoption.

### `ENO-WOH-73:1-8 + 74:1-12` — Aramaic-fuller-than-Ge'ez lunar tables

**Issue:** The Aramaic 4Q208 + 4Q209 DSS fragments preserve detailed monthly lunar-disk-fraction tables (the 49-unit fractional grid) that the Ge'ez tradition has heavily abbreviated. The witness-fullness-inversion is unusual within the 1 Enoch corpus.

**Resolution:** Created overlay entry `aramaic-fuller-than-geez-lunar-tables-4q208-convention`. The WoH translation preserves the Ge'ez compressed-summary as the surface text (the Aramaic detailed lunar tables are highly technical computational-data that does not translate naturally into the WoH narrative-translation register); the witness-situation is flagged in the per-translation overlay and in chapter-level commentary.

### `ENO-WOH-78:1-2` — corrupted Ge'ez sun-and-moon names

**Issue:** The Ge'ez tradition has preserved sun-names ('Oryares', 'Tomos', 'Asonja') and moon-names (Asanaya, Abal, Banase, Erae) in unrecognizable-corrupted forms. Multiple speculative reconstructions have been proposed but none has achieved scholarly consensus.

**Resolution:** Created overlay entry `corrupted-geez-sun-moon-names-78-1-2-preserve-verbatim-no-emendation-convention`. The WoH translation preserves the corrupted Ge'ez forms verbatim in transliteration without proposing emendations. The corrupted-names are part of the source-text-historical-witness situation; emendation would require speculation about lost-original-forms that current scholarship does not warrant.

### `ENO-WOH-82:11` — five-name list where the system expects four

**Issue:** The Ge'ez witness preserves a five-name season-leader list (Melkiel, Elmeel, Helemmelek, Meleyal, Narel) where the four-quarter system expects four canonical names. The fifth name (Elmeel) is likely a scribal-doublet inflation.

**Resolution:** Folded into commentary at 1 En 82:11. The WoH translation preserves the Ge'ez verbatim per the per-verse layered-witness convention; the canonical-four reconstruction (Melkiel, Helemmelek, Meleyal, Narel per Charles 1912 + Nickelsburg-VanderKam 2012) is documented in the commentary as the modern-critical scholarly reading without being adopted as the surface text.

### `ENO-WOH-82:20` — Ge'ez ending abrupt; Aramaic 4Q211 continuation

**Issue:** The Ge'ez witness ends abruptly at the summer-quarter (Helemmelek) section; the Aramaic 4Q211 fragments preserve a continuation beyond the Ge'ez ending. Major scholarly significance (Milik 1976).

**Resolution:** Folded into commentary at 1 En 82:20. The WoH translation restricts to verses with Ge'ez parallel; the 4Q211 extra-Ge'ez continuation is documented in the commentary as evidence for the broader Aramaic-fuller-than-Ge'ez pattern. No appendix-extension paragraphs were added in this Editor pass; the human reviewer may evaluate whether to seed extra-Ge'ez Aramaic-only appendix material in a future Editor pass.
