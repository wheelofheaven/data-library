## The 11 new three-way bidirectional sibling-clusters

This chapter establishes the **project's first three-way cross-language sibling-clusters** (Sumerian + future Akkadian + Hebrew). The Hebrew↔Sumerian links are wired now; the Akkadian future-links are flagged for Atrahasīs/Gilgamesh-XI extension. Each cluster preserves the **etymological-cognate-vs-comparative-mythology distinction** in its rationale (Hebrew-Akkadian are Semitic cognates; Sumerian-Hebrew are comparative-mythology only since Sumerian is a language isolate).

The 11 clusters:
1. **Flood vocabulary** — Sumerian *a-ma-ru* ↔ Hebrew *mabbul* (cross-link to Akkadian *abūbu* flagged for Atrahasīs)
2. **Ark/flood-vessel** — Sumerian *ma2-gur* ↔ Hebrew *tevah* (cross-link to Akkadian *eleppu* flagged)
3. **Boat-window** — Sumerian *ab-BUR2* ↔ Hebrew *chalon*/*tzohar* (Akkadian *aptu* flagged)
4. **Oath-by-life-of-heaven-and-earth** — Sumerian *zi an-na zi ki-a* ↔ Hebrew `chei-x-oath-by-the-life-formula` (Gen 47)
5. **Apotheosis (*til3 diĝir-gin7*)** — Sumerian Ziusudra-immortalized ↔ Hebrew Hanokh (Gen 5:24) + Eliyahu (2 Kgs 2:11) (Akkadian *kīma ilāni* Gilgamesh XI flagged)
6. **Eternal life (*zi da-ri2*)** — Sumerian ↔ Hebrew (Akkadian *balāṭu dārû* flagged)
7. **Prostration-gesture (*giri17 ki su-ub*)** — Sumerian ↔ Hebrew *shachah apayim artzah* (Akkadian *appa labānu* flagged)
8. **Destructive-storm (*im-ḫul*)** — Sumerian ↔ Hebrew *ruach ra'ah* (Akkadian *imḫullu* — Marduk's evil-wind weapon — flagged)
9. **Eastern-paradise** — Sumerian **Dilmun** ↔ Hebrew Gan-Eden (*mi-qedem*) (Gilgamesh XI *ina rūqi* flagged)
10. **Divine-assembly-decree-irrevocability (*di-til-la*)** — Sumerian ↔ Hebrew *sod YHWH* (Akkadian *puḫrum* flagged)
11. **Post-flood-thanksgiving-sacrifice** — Sumerian ↔ Hebrew Gen 8:20-21 (Atrahasis III.v + Gilgamesh XI.155-160 flagged)
