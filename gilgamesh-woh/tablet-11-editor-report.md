## Speculative entries requiring sign-off

**None.** All 12 new central entries and all 6 new overlay entries added in
this pass are `claim_type: direct`. The reasoning: every new entry
documents a philologically-attested lexeme, an attested cross-corpus
parallel from named-scholarship, or an editorial rendering-convention.
No interpretive synthesis goes beyond what is supported by Foster 2005,
George 2003, Heidel 1946, Lambert-Millard 1969, Tigay 1982, Kvanvig 2011,
Westermann 1984, Wenham 1987 WBC, Bauer 2018, Reynolds 2010, or the
project's prior central-glossary pattern.

Two new central entries deploy the **acknowledgment-without-endorsement
framework** for popular-fringe ET-civilization reception (the Anunnaki
and the Seven Sages); these are direct rather than speculative because
they document the popular-fringe reading as a reception-phenomenon
without endorsing it as source-textually warranted. The framework
parallels the existing project precedent (Shi'ur Qomah parasangs;
prior Anunnaki-and-apkallu treatments in Adapa-WoH and Sumerian-Flood-Story).

endorsement.

## What to flag for human review

1. **The Anunnaki and Seven Sages framing.** Both entries are
   `claim_type: direct` because they document the mainstream-Assyriological
   reading; the acknowledgment-without-endorsement framework is invoked
   to handle popular-fringe reception. Human reviewer should confirm
   that this framing matches the project's intended posture (parallel
   to Shi'ur Qomah parasangs precedent).
2. **The cross-corpus Mt Nimush / Ararat / al-Jūdī clustering.** The
   new central entry clusters three traditions' mountain-landings as
   *structural-parallel*, not *identification*. The cosmic-mountain
   bidirectional links to Mt Ṣapunu and Mt Sinai/Horeb extend the
   cluster further. Human reviewer should confirm the bidirectional
   wiring matches the project's cosmic-mountain treatment.
3. **The Adapa-Gilgamesh-Eden structural-inversion entry**
   (`utnapishtim-deification-vs-adapa-immortality-loss-cross-corpus`).
   This is the most-developed three-narrative comparative-mythology
   entry in the new pass; the framing follows named-scholarship
   (Buccellati 1973, Hallo 1969, Kvanvig 2011, Heidel 1942) but
   produces a synthesis that should receive human review.
4. **The flood-causation four-tradition entry**
   (`flood-causation-divine-council-gilgamesh-atrahasis-genesis-qur-an-cross-corpus`).
   This documents an arc of theological-moralization across four
   traditions (Mesopotamian → Atrahasis → Hebrew → Qur'anic) per named
   comparative-mythology scholarship. The arc-claim is not a normative
   ranking; the entry's disavowals make this explicit. Human reviewer
   should confirm the framing.
5. **The seven-loaves catalogue (XI:217-222)** has commentary at only
   the opening verse. The day-by-day catalogue could merit per-day
   commentary; the Editor judged the literal-catalogue self-evident.
6. **The Ur-Shanabi sequence (XI:241-264)** has minimal commentary;
   the boatman-purification ritual could merit additional cross-corpus
   treatment (Hebrew priestly-purification rituals; the wider ANE
   ritual-purification cluster). Not added in this pass — judged
   non-load-bearing for the flood-narrative core.
