# Isaiah wave I-5 (chapters 28, 30, 31, 33, 34, 35) — orchestrator rulings for Translators and Editors

Waves I-1, I-3 and I-4 are signed off and merged (overlay **3.0.0**, central **4.0.0**). Every entry they created is a
house rule; read the overlay before deciding anything. If a ruling is indefensible at a particular verse, keep it in the
line and escalate it.

1. **Fixed by earlier waves (apply, do not re-decide):**
   - hôy "Woe" (28:1; 30:1; 31:1; 33:1); hā-ʾāḏôn "the Sovereign"; ʾăḏōnāy "the Lord"; ʾăḏōnāy YHWH "the Lord YHWH";
     YHWH ṣᵉḇāʾôṯ "YHWH of hosts".
   - ʾāḏām / ʾîš → "humankind … everyone"; generic human nouns gender-neutral; specific male groups keep "men"
     (`adam-humankind-ish-man-isaiah`; wave I-4 rule 13).
   - "glory" = kāḇôḏ strictly, also of humans (`kavod-nondivine-glory-isaiah`); "splendor" = hāḏār; "honor" =
     tip̄ʾereṯ (`tiferet-honor-isaiah`); "beauty" = ṣᵉḇî (`tsevi-beauty-isaiah`); "majesty" = gāʾôn of God, "pride" =
     gāʾôn of human arrogance (`gaon-pride-human-arrogance-isaiah`).
   - "Cush", "Aram", "the Nile" (singular yᵉʾôr), nāhār "the River" = Euphrates; nēs "signal"; šôp̄ār "shofar";
     sign-names transliterated; "Mighty God" for ʾēl gibbôr.
   - Masoretic text translated; qere followed where marked (`mt-qere-textual-policy-isaiah`).
2. **Forward commitments made by earlier entries (binding):**
   - **28:10, 13** ṣaw lāṣāw … qaw lāqāw → "line upon line" per `qav-qav-line-isaiah` (18:2, 7). If the Editor departs,
     18:2 and 18:7 must be revisited and the departure escalated.
   - **28:22** kālāh wᵉneḥĕrāṣāh → "a full end, and that determined" per central `kalah-venecheratzah-…` (10:23).
   - **28:16** the tested, precious cornerstone (cf. 1 Pet 2:6; Rom 9:33) — NT reception in commentary only.
3. **Consistency with stable neighbours.** 29, 32 and 24–27 are stable: match "Woe, Ariel" style, the 32:1 king-in-
   righteousness diction, and 24–27 vocabulary. 30:7 Rahab and 30:33 Topheth; 34:14 lîlîṯ (render by philology, flag;
   the lens-salient reading of Lilith stays out of the line); 34:4 "all the host of the heavens … the skies shall be rolled up like
   a scroll" (cf. Rev 6:13–14) — CORRECTED: central `shamayim` is "skies" (stable 1:2, 13:5, 13:10, 13:13, 40:22);
   "heavens" only inside the fixed phrase "the host of the heavens" (central `tzeva-ha-shamayim-…`, register rule).
4. **Lens.** Lens-neutral English in every line. Wheel of Heaven readings only in a final commentary paragraph labelled
   speculative, quoting the canon only after checking the data-library text and saying plainly whether the canon cites
   the verse (TBWTT cites Isaiah only in chs 3 and 5 — check which Isaiah verses). Founder policy keeps such notes.
5. **Citations.** NJPS = "Tanakh: The Holy Scriptures, published by JPS" (1985) on Sefaria, not the Gender-Sensitive
   Edition (`/api/v3/texts/Isaiah.N?version=english|all`; Hebrew versification). Cite NRSV, LXX, 1QIsaᵃ, lexica or
   commentators only when checked; otherwise "not verified". Never present a JPS 1917 reading as ASV.
6. **Files.** Translators: `chapter-N.json` in the shape of `chapter-23.json` but WITHOUT paragraph `translit` (the
   orchestrator generates it); status "draft", version "0.1.0", glossaryVersion "4.0.0", overlayGlossaryVersion
   "3.0.0". Editors: `wave-i5-ch<N>-overlay-diff.json` / `-central-diff.json` (list of ops: version / add / modify with
   appliesToAdded, optional changes, optional rationaleAppend); propose overlay 3.1.0, central 4.1.0. Never edit a shared
   glossary; never touch other waves' files or other chapters.

7. **lʿg "stammering"** (ruled during drafting): 28:11 laʿăḡê śāp̄āh and 33:19 nilʿaḡ lāšôn share one English root — "stammering" (JPS 1917 at 33:19); the chapter 28 Editor adds the entry, the chapter 33 Editor extends it.
