# Isaiah wave I-6 (chapters 36, 37, 38, 39, 41) — orchestrator rulings for Translators and Editors

Waves I-1, I-3, I-4 and I-5 are signed off and merged (overlay **4.0.0**, central **5.0.0**). Every entry they created is a
house rule; read the overlay before deciding anything. If a ruling is indefensible at a particular verse, keep it in the
line and escalate it.

1. **Fixed by earlier waves (apply, do not re-decide):**
   - hôy "Woe" (28:1; 30:1; 31:1; 33:1); hā-ʾāḏôn "the Sovereign"; ʾăḏōnāy "the Lord"; ʾăḏōnāy YHWH "the Lord YHWH";
     YHWH ṣᵉḇāʾôṯ "YHWH of hosts".
   - ʾāḏām / ʾîš → "humankind … everyone"; generic human nouns gender-neutral; specific male groups keep "men"
     (`adam-humankind-ish-man-isaiah`; wave I-4 rule 13).
   - "glory" = kāḇôḏ strictly, also of humans (`kavod-nondivine-glory-isaiah`); "splendor" = hāḏār; "honor" =
     tip̄ʾereṯ (`tiferet-honor-isaiah`); "beauty" = ṣᵉḇî (`tsevi-beauty-isaiah`); "majesty" = gāʾôn of God, "pride" =
     gāʾôn of human arrogance (`gaon-pride-human-arrogance-isaiah`).
   - "Cush", "Aram", "the Nile" (singular yᵉʾôr), nāhār "the River" = Euphrates; nēs "signal"; šôp̄ār "shofar";
     sign-names transliterated; "Mighty God" for ʾēl gibbôr.
   - Masoretic text translated; qere followed where marked (`mt-qere-textual-policy-isaiah`).
2. **Forward commitments and wave I-5 house rules (binding):** hôḏ "grandeur"; pāḏâ "ransom" beside gʾl, else "redeem" (central goel); lʿg "stammering"; šāmayim "skies" ("heavens" only in "host of the heavens"); ʾĕnôš "mortal"; ʾaddîr "mighty"; he/his kept after gender-neutral nouns; `ruin-fauna-isaiah` names. Earlier forward items (done):
   - **28:10, 13** ṣaw lāṣāw … qaw lāqāw → "line upon line" per `qav-qav-line-isaiah` (18:2, 7). If the Editor departs,
     18:2 and 18:7 must be revisited and the departure escalated.
   - **28:22** kālāh wᵉneḥĕrāṣāh → "a full end, and that determined" per central `kalah-venecheratzah-…` (10:23).
   - **28:16** the tested, precious cornerstone (cf. 1 Pet 2:6; Rom 9:33) — NT reception in commentary only.
3. **Isaiah 36–39 = 2 Kings 18:13–20:19 (parallel prose).** 2 Kings 18–20 is NOT yet in the corpus, so Isaiah sets the shared
   wording: render each verse on its own Hebrew, and record in commentary where 2 Kgs differs (2 Kgs 18:14–16 has no Isaiah
   parallel; Isa 38:9–20 the Psalm of Hezekiah has none in Kings). Use ASV place/person spellings (Rabshakeh, Eliakim son of
   Hilkiah, Shebna, Joah son of Asaph, Lachish, Libnah, Tirhakah king of Cush — `cush-names-isaiah` lists 37:9). 37:16
   "YHWH of hosts, God of Israel, who sits enthroned above the cherubim" (check central cherubim entry); 37:36 malʾaḵ YHWH —
   decide "messenger" vs "angel" against `lexical-cruxes-isaiah-33` (which binds only translated sites; 37:36 was explicitly
   left open) and escalate; 38:8 the shadow on the steps of Ahaz going back ten steps — lens-salient, philological line;
   38:10–20 the psalm: Sheol, "the land of the living". 37:22–35 the taunt over Sennacherib (poetry).
   Chapter 41: the trial of the nations; 41:8 "Israel my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraham my friend" (CORRECTED: zeraʿ is "seed" in the stable 43:5, 44:3, 45:19, 25, 48:19);
   41:14 "worm Jacob"; gōʾēl "Redeemer" (central goel); 41:2, 25 the one raised from the east/north (Cyrus, unnamed) —
   lens-neutral; stable 40, 42–45 vocabulary governs.
4. **Lens.** Lens-neutral English in every line. Wheel of Heaven readings only in a final commentary paragraph labelled
   speculative, quoting the canon only after checking the data-library text and saying plainly whether the canon cites the
   verse. A speculative note needs a premise a source supports — the wave I-5 30:26 note was dropped because its premise had
   no source; do not write one without that.
5. **Citations.** NJPS = "Tanakh: The Holy Scriptures, published by JPS" (1985) on Sefaria, not the Gender-Sensitive Edition
   (Hebrew versification). Cite NRSV, LXX, 1QIsaᵃ, lexica or commentators only when checked; otherwise "not verified". Never
   present a JPS 1917 reading as ASV. Quote cross-references only after checking that the cited verse has the word claimed.
6. **Files.** Translators: `chapter-N.json` in the shape of `chapter-35.json` but WITHOUT paragraph `translit`, reviewerReport
   or signOff; status "draft", version "0.1.0", glossaryVersion "5.0.0", overlayGlossaryVersion "4.0.0". Editors:
   `wave-i6-ch<N>-overlay-diff.json` / `-central-diff.json` (list of ops: version / add / modify with appliesToAdded,
   optional changes, optional rationaleAppend); propose overlay 4.1.0, central 5.1.0. Never edit a shared glossary; never
   touch other waves' files or other chapters.
7. **raḇ-šāqēh** (ruled during drafting): an Assyrian title, rendered "the Rabshakeh" with the article at every site (36:2, 4,
   11–13, 22; 37:4, 8), matching "the Tartan" at 20:1. The chapter 36 Editor adds the entry; chapter 37 extends it.
8. **sᵉp̄ārîm (37:14; 39:1)** (ruled during editing): one noun, "letter", number by each verse's grammar (37:14 "the letter",
   singular suffixes; 39:1 "letters"); a diplomatic sēp̄er is outside `sefer-sealed-scroll-isaiah-29`.
