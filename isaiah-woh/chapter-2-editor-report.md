## Escalated rulings (founder sign-off by name)

### `adam-humankind-ish-man-isaiah` (wave-wide ruling: ʾāḏām/ʾîš pairs)

**Source:** וַיִּשַּׁח אָדָם וַיִּשְׁפַּל־אִישׁ (2:9; refrain at 5:15)
**WoH choice:** "So humankind is bowed down, and man is brought low"; generic *ʾāḏām* is "humankind" at 2:11, 17, 20 and 22, and *ʾănāšîm* is "men".
**Why escalated:** the wave ruling requires it. The rendering is `direct`: it reads the two nouns as a synonymous pair, as NRSV, NJPS, Wildberger and Williamson do, and it matches what the book already prints at 29:19 and 45:12. ASV instead reads a social contrast ("the mean man … the great man").
**Side effect:** at 2:22 the Hebrew singular pronouns are kept, giving "humankind, who has breath in his nostrils". That is slightly awkward English. The alternative is NRSV's "mortals … their".
**Recommendation:** ship.

### 2:20 word division (`lexical-cruxes-isaiah-2`, reported under `mt-qere-textual-policy-isaiah`)

**Issue:** The Leningrad Codex/WLC prints לַחְפֹּר פֵּרוֹת as two words, "to dig holes". The WLC encoding has no ketiv/qere note here, so this is not a qere site. The line follows the one-word reading *laḥăp̄arpērôṯ* "to the moles". That reading is found in many manuscripts and the versions and is recorded in BDB and the BHS apparatus; ASV, NRSV and NJPS all translate it.
**Why escalated:** the consonants are unchanged, but the word division departs from the base text. The policy entry `mt-qere-textual-policy-isaiah` does not yet name this kind of departure.
**Options:** A, the one-word reading (taken). B, MT division: "to dig holes, and to the bats".
**Recommendation:** ship A, and add the suggested addendum (in the overlay diff note) to the policy rationale.

### hā-ʾāḏôn → "the Lord"; hôy → "Woe"

Neither occurs in Isaiah 2, so nothing applies here.

## Flagged for review (not sign-off-gated)

- **2:16 text change**: "pleasant imagery" (ASV) becomes "all the beautiful vessels" (`sekhiyyot-hachemdah-vessels-isaiah-2`, `inferred`). The line now reads with HALOT, NRSV and NJPS: an Egyptian ship-word, in parallel with "ships of Tarshish". "Vessels" is used instead of NRSV's "craft", which could be read as aircraft. This is a lens-sensitive site, and the change moves away from the Translator's cautious default, so the Reviewer should look at it.
- **2:10, 19, 21 text change**: "the glory of his majesty" becomes "the splendor of his majesty" (`hadar-splendor-isaiah`). This keeps "glory" for *kāḇôḏ*, and the house already prints *hāḏār* as "splendor" (53:2) and *gāʾôn* as "majesty" (24:14). Cross-chapter note for the Isa 4 Editor: 4:2 prints "splendor" for *tip̄ʾereṯ*, which collides with this concordance.
- **2:19, 21 text change**: "to shake the earth mightily" (ASV) becomes "to terrify the earth" (NRSV; BDB/HALOT *ʿāraṣ*; Isa 8:12–13, 29:23, 47:12).
