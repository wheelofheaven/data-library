## Unresolved editorial questions

None are unresolved. The rulings below are recorded in the diffs, but they change the line against ASV, so they are listed here for explicit review.

### `ISA-9:5`: throne-name punctuation

**Issue:** The accents divide פֶּלֶא (telisha gedolah) from יוֹעֵץ (pashta), which ASV and KJV follow with five names.
**Ruling:** "Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace" (four paired titles, NRSV), joined against the accents. The grounds are the two-word structure of the other three names and 28:29 *hip̄lîʾ ʿēṣāh*. The accent division is recorded in commentary and in `yeled-yullad-throne-names-isaiah-9-5` (claim type: inferred).
**Options considered:** ASV five names; NRSV four pairs; NJPS sentence-name; transliteration.
**Why listed:** It departs from both the accents and ASV. The NJPS wording was not verified.

### `ISA-9:5–6`: tense

**Ruling:** The forms are rendered as English perfects: "has been born … has been given … has come upon his shoulder … he has been named". The future is kept at 9:6 *taʿăśeh* "will do this". ASV's prophetic-perfect futures are recorded in commentary. *miśrāh* stays "the government" (ASV); "authority" is in commentary.

### `ISA-9:7–20`: poem tense (owned by this Editor under wave ruling 8)

**Ruling:** The strokes are past throughout.
- The *wayyiqtol* forms are simple past.
- The yiqtol forms, participles and the weqatal frame verbs inside the narrative (9:7–8) take the past of their context.
- The negated perfects of a continuing state (9:12) and the fixed refrain are English perfects.
- The quoted boast (9:9) and the nominal gloss (9:14) keep their own tenses.
- The waw-consecutive is "So" (9:10, 13), and *ʿal-kēn* is "Therefore" (9:16).

The ruling is recorded as overlay `poem-tense-outstretched-hand-isaiah-9` (claim type: inferred). It is consistent with 5:25 and with the rationale of the refrain entry. It does not bind 10:1–4 beyond the refrain at 10:4.
**Weakest point:** 9:8 *wᵉyāḏᵉʿû* (weqatal). ASV's future "shall know" remains possible and is reported in commentary.

### Other line changes against ASV (all in `lexical-cruxes-isaiah-9`, claim type: direct)

- 9:4 "every boot of the one who tramps" (BDB *sᵉʾôn*; NRSV), replacing "armor of the armed man".
- 9:7 "against Jacob … fell on Israel", replacing "into".
- 9:11 "Aram from the east and the Philistines from the west" (NRSV; cf. 2:6), replacing "before … behind".
- 9:16 "godless" (wave ruling 5), replacing "profane".
- 9:20 "Manasseh devoured Ephraim": the verb is supplied from 9:19, because the object markers require it (NRSV).
