## 26:19 — the marquee crux (detailed)

**Surface (governed by the two new overlay entries):**
> "Your dead shall live; my corpses shall rise. Awake and sing for joy,
> you who dwell in the dust! For your dew is a dew of lights, and the
> earth will give birth to the shades."

The Translator's draft ("my dead bodies shall arise … the earth shall
cast forth the shades") was changed at two points, each now governed by
a glossary entry (per hard-rule #7): `nᵉḇēlāṯî yᵉqûmûn` → "my corpses
shall rise" (precise `nᵉḇēlāh` = corpse; collective for the 1cs/3mp
number-crux), and `tappîl` → "the earth will give birth to the shades"
(ties the verb to the childbirth-imagery of 26:17–18; NRSV register).
Both are within the standard scholarly range (ASV "cast forth" noted as
the equal alternative in the entry + commentary).

**Four nested decisions, all resolved to MT, no lens imposed:**

1. **Resurrection reading** — MT surface "your dead shall live" is
   `direct`. Whether literal individual bodily resurrection or
   national/corporate-restoration metaphor is left OPEN. The
   bodily-resurrection reading + the Dan 12:2 / Ezek 37 tradition-cluster
   are apparatus-only (commentary + central entry governance). Sources:
   J. Day, *God's Conflict with the Dragon and the Sea* (1985); J. D.
   Levenson, *Resurrection and the Restoration of Israel* (2006);
   Wildberger; Blenkinsopp.
2. **`ṭal ʾôrōṯ` "dew of lights"** — `ʾôrōṯ` = "lights" (plural of
   `ʾôrāh`, luminous/life-giving dew; NRSV "dew of light"), retained
   over the homograph "herbs/mallows" (2 Kgs 4:39; ASV/KJV "dew of
   herbs"), which is disclosed. → overlay `tal-orot-dew-of-lights-isaiah-26`.
3. **`rᵉp̄āʾîm` (again, cf. 26:14)** — "the shades," here cast forth
   ALIVE (the inverse of 26:14). Same shades-sense, NOT giant-Rephaim.
   Governed by the shared `rephaim-shades-dead-isaiah-26`.
4. **Number crux `nᵉḇēlāṯî` (1cs sg.) / `yᵉqûmûn` (3mp)** — rendered
   collectively "my corpses shall rise," MT retained against the
   emendation `nᵉḇēlāṯām` "their bodies" (NRSV/RSV). `tappîl` (Hiphil of
   `nāp̄al`) → "give birth to." → overlay `resurrection-line-yichyu-metekha-isaiah-26`.

These are the chapter's lens-discipline records. **Do NOT apply.**

- **`refaim-giant-peoples`** — NOT extended to ISA-26:14 or 26:19. These

  ISA-26:14 or 26:19. Governs the antediluvian giant-warrior chain; the
  "shades" are the ordinary dead, not warriors/giants. Applying it would
  be the lens move the task flags.

  God"), per the 53:4 / 24:15 / 25:1,9 precedent chain.

  (those verses use `rᵉp̄āʾîm`, not `šᵉʾôl`; Sheol is referenced in
