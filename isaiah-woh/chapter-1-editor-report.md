## Escalated rulings and unresolved editorial questions

### `ISA-1:24` — hā-ʾāḏôn rendered "the Lord" (wave-wide ruling, ESCALATE)

**Issue:** The English line gives the articular common noun hā-ʾāḏôn the same rendering as the title ʾăḏōnāy ("the Lord", 6:1).
NRSV and NJPS distinguish the two with "the Sovereign".
**Options considered:**
- A. "the Lord", with a note at each site (this is the ruling, and it is applied).
- B. "the Sovereign" (NRSV, NJPS).
- C. "the Master".

**Why escalating:** The wave ruling asks for founder confirmation.
The choice applies book-wide: 1:24 and 3:1 in this wave, and later 10:16, 33 and 19:4.
Entry: overlay `ha-adon-articular-title-isaiah` (claim_type `direct`).

### `ISA-1:31` — ûp̄ōʿălô "his work" vs "its maker"

**Issue:** The Masoretic vowels of וּפֹעֲלוֹ match the suffixed Qal participle ("his maker"; compare Job 36:3 לְפֹעֲלִי "my Maker").
The noun with suffix is normally pointed פָּעֳלוֹ.
Under the book's MT-pointing policy (applied at 1:12), the pointing could favour "and its maker a spark", with the idol and its maker in view.
ASV and NRSV give "his work". NJPS reportedly reads the maker.
**Options considered:**
- A. "his work" (ASV, NRSV; kept).
- B. "its maker" (follows the MT vowels).

**Why escalating:** The line keeps a noun reading that the pointing may not support.
The Editor could not confirm the lexica's (BDB, HALOT) classification of this site without the reference shelf.
The Reviewer should check, and should adopt B under `lexical-cruxes-isaiah-1` if the participle analysis holds.
