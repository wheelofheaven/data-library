## Lens-discipline records (for the reviewer's attention)

### 1. TOP FLAG — `ṣᵉḇāʾ ham-mārôm ba-mārôm` "the host of the height on high" at 24:21 (`tzeva-hamarom-host-of-height-isaiah-24`)

**Decision:** the text renders **"the host of the height on high"** (ASV "the host of the high ones"; JPS "the host of the high heaven") and **identifies no referent.** The plain "host of heaven / host of the height" is `direct`. The rebellious-watchers / divine-council reading, the astral-host reading, and the angelic-patron-princes reading are all **inferred** and are set out in commentary as hedged, apparatus-only options — **none is imposed on the line.** The entry is explicit that this `ṣᵉḇāʾ` (a host YHWH *punishes*) is grammatically and referentially **DISTINCT** from the divine title `YHWH ṣᵉḇāʾôṯ` "YHWH of hosts" of v23 (where YHWH *commands* the hosts, overlay `yhwh-tzevaot-hosts-isaiah`).

**Cross-corpus, deliberately NOT applied:** the later Second-Temple imprisonment-of-cosmic-rebels tradition (1 Enoch 18:13–16; 21:1–10 — the central `fallen-stars-prison-1-enoch-18-21-jude-6-2-pet-2-4-cross-corpus` entry) reads a heavenly host bound and imprisoned, and v22 continues with exactly that imprisonment. The resonance is noted in commentary, but **the central `fallen-stars-prison` entry is NOT extended to ISA-24:21/22** — adding those refIds would fold Isaiah 24 into the confinement-of-cosmic-rebels *framework*, i.e. would promote the inferred rebellious-host identification into the governed set. Accuracy-before-lens forbids that. Recorded as a CENTRAL non-extension below.

### 2. `ʾĕlōhê yiśrāʾēl` "the God of Israel" at 24:15 (`elohei-yisrael-god-of-israel-isaiah-24`)

**Decision:** rendered **"the God of Israel"** at the standard surface; the corpus "the Elohim" convention (central `elohim-as-translation`) is **deliberately NOT applied** to this construct divine epithet, exactly on the Isa 53:4 precedent. The entry's own `wohChoice` is "the Elohim," which cannot govern a bound epithet whose natural English is "God of Israel"; **ISA-24:15 is NOT added to the central `elohim-as-translation` appliesTo.** Recorded as a CENTRAL non-extension below.

### 3. `ʾărubbôṯ mimmārôm` "windows on high" at 24:18 — the Flood echo (`arubot-mimarom-windows-on-high-isaiah-24`)

**Decision:** rendered **"the windows on high"** (ASV/JPS); the deliberate Gen 7:11 Flood-reversal echo is carried in commentary. The lexeme `ʾărubbāh` (H699) is the same as at Gen 7:11, but the central `arubot-ha-shamayim` entry's `wohChoice` is the lens-tilted **"floodgates of the skies,"** which is *not* what we render here, and the construct differs (`mimmārôm` "from on high," not `haš-šāmayim` "of the skies"). Per the same non-extension logic as `elohim-as-translation` @ 53:4, **`arubot-ha-shamayim` is NOT extended to ISA-24:18** (wohChoice ≠ line). Recorded as a CENTRAL non-extension below.

### 4. `bᵉrîṯ ʿôlām` covenant referent at 24:5 (`chanef-broken-everlasting-covenant-isaiah-24`)

**Decision:** rendered to the standard surface **"the everlasting covenant"** (central `brit-olam`, extended to ISA-24:5 — see CENTRAL diff); `ḥānᵉp̄āh` "lies defiled." The **referent** of the covenant (Noahic universal ordinance of Gen 9:16 / Sinai-Torah / cosmic order-of-creation) is **left unidentified** in the line. The universal-Noahic reading is canon-adjacent and is noted as the reading most at home in a world-judgment, but the Hebrew names no referent and the translation does not resolve it.

### 5. `qiryaṯ-tōhû` "city of chaos" at 24:10 (`qiryat-tohu-city-of-chaos-isaiah-24`)

**Decision:** rendered **"the city of chaos"** (JPS/NRSV; ASV "city of confusion"). `tōhû` is the same lexeme as Gen 1:2 `tōhû wā-ḇōhû` and Isa 34:11, but the central `tohu-vavohu` entry governs the Genesis **pair** ("wasteland and emptiness") and is **not applied** to this bare singular construct; the cross-corpus resonance is carried in commentary. Recorded as a CENTRAL non-extension below.

The task brief flagged that **Wildberger's *Isaiah 13–27* (Continental Commentary, Fortress, 1997) DOES cover chapters 24–27** — unlike his commentary set, which does not reach 40–55. That is correct: Wildberger is a valid, non-fabricated authority for this chapter, and is cited throughout the commentary and rationales alongside **Blenkinsopp** (*Isaiah 1–39*, AB 19, 2000), **Kaiser** (*Isaiah 13–39*, OTL), and — for the host-of-heaven/divine-council material — **Mullen** (*The Divine Council in Canaanite and Early Hebrew Literature*, HSM 24, 1980) and **F.
