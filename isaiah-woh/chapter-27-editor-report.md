## 4. Framework/speculative lens claims requiring human sign-off

### `ISA-27:1` — the WoH Theomachy-convergence synthesis (commentary-only)

**Source:** לִוְיָתָן נָחָשׁ בָּרִחַ … נָחָשׁ עֲקַלָּתוֹן … הַתַּנִּין אֲשֶׁר בַּיָּם
**Where it lives:** the `notes.official` commentary on ISA-27:1 — a single,
explicitly **fenced** paragraph (blockquote, labelled "Wheel of Heaven
reading (interpretive layer, beyond the text)").
**The claim:** that Isaiah's serpent, the Ugaritic Lôtanu, the Babylonian
Tiamat, and the Indo-European dragon-cycles are convergent mythic
compressions of remembered conflict among the Elohim (a cross-cultural
**Theomachy-convergence**).
**Why escalated:** this is a `framework`-level synthesis (canon-adjacent,
not endorsed by mainstream scholarship). It goes beyond both the verse and
the comparative philology. It is **not** encoded in any glossary entry and
**not** carried in the line — the overlay `leviathan-nachash-chaoskampf-isaiah-27`
entry is `claim_type=direct` and confines this synthesis to the apparatus.
Per the hard rule that lens lives in the apparatus, and because commentary
should surface lexical evidence rather than argue the cosmology, the claim
is quarantined, hedged, and labelled as the project's interpretive layer.
**Recommendation:** **SHIP as commentary**, retaining the fence and the
hedge. The confirmable spine of the note — the Isaiah/Ugaritic epithet-pair
cognate (Day 1985; KTU 1.5 I / 1.3 III) with Tsumura 2005 as the
counter-voice — is a well-attested scholarly datum and ships without
qualification. **Human reviewer: please confirm this framework paragraph
by name.**

**No `claim_type=speculative` glossary entries were created in this pass.**
Because the framework claim lives in commentary and no speculative *entry*
exists, hard-rule-5 does not block the advance to `editor-review`; the
paragraph is nonetheless flagged here as the chapter's accountability
surface.
