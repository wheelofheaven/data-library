## Resolutions of the 10 editorial questions

### 1. `ISA-25:1` — `ʾĕlōhay` 'my God' + `ʾĕmûnāh ʾōmen`
**Decision: RATIFY the default.** `YHWH ʾĕlōhay ʾattāh` -> 'YHWH, you are my
God'; the suffixed `ʾĕlōhay` (1cs) rendered 'my God', the corpus 'the Elohim'
convention (central `elohim-as-translation`, Genesis-1-scoped) deliberately
**not** applied to a vocative doxology, per the Isa 53:4 / 24:15 precedent.
`ʾĕmûnāh ʾōmen` -> 'faithfulness and truth' (ASV/JPS), the intensified single
notion ('sure faithfulness') noted in commentary. Folded into overlay entries
`pele-etzot-emunah-hymn-vocabulary-isaiah-25` (peleʾ / ʿēṣôṯ mē-rāḥôq / ʾĕmûnāh
ʾōmen) and `elohai-elohenu-doxology-isaiah-25` (the `ʾĕlōhay`/`ʾĕlōhênû`
non-application, shared with Q7).

### 2. `ISA-25:5` — `zᵉmîr ʿārîṣîm yaʿăneh` double crux
**Decision: RATIFY the default.** 'the song of the ruthless is brought low'
(`zᵉmîr` = 'song', √zmr I; `yaʿăneh` = 'brought low', √ʿnh II). The 'branch/
pruning' (√zmr II) and 'is answered/silenced' (√ʿnh I) readings recorded as
alternatives. `zārîm` -> 'strangers'. Folded into overlay entry
`zemir-aritzim-song-crux-isaiah-25`.

### 3. `ISA-25:6` — mountain banquet + `YHWH ṣᵉḇāʾôṯ` (FLAG)
**Decision: RATIFY the default; extension ratified.** 'And on this mountain
YHWH of hosts will make for all the peoples a feast of fat things …'. The
existing overlay entry `yhwh-tzevaot-hosts-isaiah` **appliesTo extended
+ISA-25:6** (in-overlay, done); the heavenly-host/divine-assembly reading of
`ṣᵉḇāʾôṯ` stays confined to that entry's apparatus, not surfaced in the line.
The eschatological/messianic-banquet reception (Matt 8:11; Luke 14:15-24;
Rev 19:9) kept as hedged commentary. New overlay entry
`mishteh-shemanim-shemarim-banquet-isaiah-25`.

### 4. `ISA-25:7` — `billaʿ` + `pᵉnê-hal-lôṭ hal-lôṭ` + `ha-massēḵāh`
**Decision: RATIFY the default.** 'he will swallow up … the face of the shroud
that shrouds all the peoples, and the veil that is spread over all the nations'.
`billaʿ` in the destroy/do-away-with sense (the catchword shared with 25:8);
`hal-lôṭ` 'shroud/covering' (√lwṭ), `massēḵāh` 'veil'; the funerary-shroud
reading (tying 25:7 to 25:8) noted as context-favoured, the epistemic-veil
reading recorded. Folded into overlay entry `lot-massekhah-shroud-veil-isaiah-25`.

### 5. `ISA-25:8` (first) — `billaʿ ham-māweṯ lā-neṣaḥ` death defeated (TOP FLAG)
**Decision: RATIFY the default; render plainly.** 'He has swallowed up death
forever' — subject YHWH, `māweṯ` rendered **abstract lower-case 'death'** (NOT
personified 'Death'), `lā-neṣaḥ` = 'forever' (MT). Both cross-corpus resonances
are laid out in commentary and kept hedged: (i) the **Ugaritic Môtu reversal**
(Death the Swallower of the Baal Cycle, KTU 1.5, himself swallowed — the verb
`blʿ` inverting Môtu's throat-iconography; J. Day, *God's Conflict with the
Dragon and the Sea*, 1985); (ii) the **NT reception / versional variant** (the
Old Greek inverts the clause, Death as subject; 1 Cor 15:54 reads the MT
direction but takes `neṣaḥ` as 'victory'; Rev 21:4 echoes the tear-wiping of the
next clause). New overlay entry `billa-hamavet-death-swallowed-isaiah-25`.
**CENTRAL diff below** extends `mot-mavet-…` appliesTo +ISA-25:8. Lens-discipline
record: see the dedicated section at the foot of this report.

### 6. `ISA-25:8` (second) — compound `ʾăḏōnāy YHWH`
**Decision: RATIFY 'the Lord YHWH' (standard surface).** The compound divine
name (`ʾăḏōnāy` + the Tetragrammaton with `ʾĕlōhîm`-vowels), rendered 'the Lord
YHWH' per the corpus compound-name convention (central `adonai-yhwh` [Ezekiel] /
`adonai-yhwh-divine-name` [Genesis], wohChoice 'Lord YHWH'), keeping the Isaiah
YHWH house-style. Previously lexically uncovered in Isaiah — the central compound
entries are book-scoped to Ezekiel/Genesis. Handled by **new overlay entry**
`adonai-yhwh-compound-isaiah-25` (not by extending the central entries; overlay
is the house pattern, cf. `adonai-standalone-title-isaiah` for the Isa 6
standalone). Kept distinct from the standalone `ʾăḏōnāy` 'the Lord' of Isa 6.

### 7. `ISA-25:9` — `ʾĕlōhênû` 'our God' doxology + `yᵉšûʿāṯô`
**Decision: RATIFY the default.** 'Behold, this is our God … This is YHWH …
let us be glad and rejoice in his salvation'. `ʾĕlōhênû` (1cp) -> 'our God', the
'the Elohim' convention deliberately not applied to a confessional acclamation
(the Isa 53:4 / 24:15 precedent, a fortiori for a first-person cry). `yᵉšûʿāṯô`
-> 'his salvation' (standard). Folded into overlay entry
`elohai-elohenu-doxology-isaiah-25` (appliesTo ISA-25:1, ISA-25:9).

### 8. `ISA-25:10` — Moab, ketiv/qere, `madmēnāh`, `yaḏ-YHWH`
**Decision: RATIFY the default; resolve to the qere.** 'as straw is trodden down
in a dung-pit' — the **qere** בְּמוֹ `bᵉmô` 'in/within' translated per the
qere-first rule, the **ketiv** במי `bᵉmê` 'in the water(s) of' (liquid-manure,
ASV) flagged. `madmēnāh` 'dung-pit/dunghill' (√dmn), with the Moabite toponym
`Madmēn` (Jer 48:2) paronomasia noted; common-noun sense taken. `yaḏ-YHWH` 'the
hand of YHWH' rendered plainly — central `yad-yhwh` (Ezekiel-scoped) **not
applied**, as in chapter 24. New overlay entry `madmenah-ketiv-qere-moab-isaiah-25`.

### 9. `ISA-25:11` — swimmer image, subject of `wᵉhišpîl`, `ʾārᵉbôṯ yāḏāyw`
**Decision: RATIFY the default.** 'as a swimmer spreads out his hands to swim;
but he will bring down his pride together with the cunning of his hands'.
`wᵉhišpîl` read actively, subject YHWH (carried from 25:10), with JPS's passive
noted; `ʾārᵉbôṯ yāḏāyw` = 'the cunning of his hands' (√ʾrb), with 'the strokes/
spreadings of his hands' recorded as an alternative. New overlay entry
`arbot-yadav-swimmer-crux-isaiah-25`.

### 10. `ISA-25:12` — fortress-demolition triplet, 2ms addressee
**Decision: RATIFY the default.** 'And the high fortress of your walls he has
brought down, laid low, brought to the ground, to the dust'. The 2ms
`ḥômōṯeḵā` 'your walls' addressed directly to Moab (apostrophe); `miḇṣar` /
`miśgāḇ` as a fortified-height pair (hendiadys recorded); the triplet `hēšaḥ` /
`hišpîl` / `higgîaʿ lā-ʾāreṣ` ending `ʿaḏ-ʿāp̄ār`. New overlay entry
`mivtzar-misgav-demolition-isaiah-25`.

## Lens-discipline records

- **ISA-25:8 (`billa-hamavet-death-swallowed-isaiah-25`) — the load-bearing
  site.** `māweṯ` is rendered abstract 'death', not personified 'Death'. The
  Ugaritic Môtu material (KTU 1.5; J. Day 1985) and the NT reception (LXX
  inversion; 1 Cor 15:54 `neṣaḥ`='victory'; Rev 21:4) are stated in commentary
  as *resonances*, hedged, and are not resolved into the MT line. The
  comparative claim is disclosed for audit, not imposed. This is the chapter's
  primary accountability surface and it stays apparatus-only.
- **ISA-25:1 / 25:9 (`elohai-elohenu-doxology-isaiah-25`).** The 'the Elohim'
  convention is deliberately withheld from first-person praise/acclamation
  (`ʾĕlōhay`/`ʾĕlōhênû` -> 'my God'/'our God'), continuing the 53:4 / 24:15
  non-extension. Accuracy-before-lens: a vocative hymn is not the Genesis-1
  narrative frame.
- **ISA-25:6 (`mishteh-…`, `yhwh-tzevaot-hosts-isaiah`).** The inclusive-banquet
  eschatology and the divine-assembly reading of `ṣᵉḇāʾôṯ` are apparatus-only;
  the line reads the plain 'YHWH of hosts … a feast … for all the peoples'.
- **ISA-25:7 (`lot-massekhah-…`).** The funerary-shroud reading (which the
  death-swallowing of 25:8 then abolishes) is noted as context-favoured but the
  line keeps the standard neutral 'shroud … veil'.
- **ISA-25:10 (`yad-YHWH`).** Central Ezekiel-scoped `yad-yhwh` not applied;
  rendered plainly.
