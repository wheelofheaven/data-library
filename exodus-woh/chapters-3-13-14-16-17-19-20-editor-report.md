selection in the Raëlian corpus after Genesis**, and the editor pass concentrated
on the four explicit Raëlian-canon-citation sites — the **pillar of cloud and
fire (Ex 13:21-22)**, the **parting of the sea (Ex 14:16-22)**, the **manna
(Ex 16:4)**, and the **Sinai theophany (Ex 19:16-19)** — plus the chapter-deep
**ʾehyeh ʾăšer ʾehyeh divine-name revelation (Ex 3:14)** and the **Decalogue
enumeration question (Ex 20)**. The pass added (i) 17 new central glossary
entries on the Exodus-specific philological / cross-corpus framework; (ii) 9
new overlay entries on Exodus-specific conventions (the ʾehyeh English-rendering
convention, the *ratsach* / *chamad* Decalogue translation-decisions, the Sinai
cloud-fire-thunder-trumpet compositional cluster, the *chamushim* / *mehuspas*
hapaxes, the *kes*/*nes* textual variant, the *yobel* shofar/jubilee-horn
distinction, the *bayn-haarbayim* dual-evening temporal convention); (iii)
per-paragraph commentary on 88 paragraphs across the 7 chapters with cross-corpus
typological flagging, explicit acknowledgment-without-endorsement at the four

## Raëlian-canon citation sites — acknowledgment-without-endorsement framework applied

The four explicit-citation sites of the Raëlian-canon engagement in *The Truth*
and *Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their Planet* are flagged in the commentary
with explicit acknowledgment-without-endorsement per the Shi'ur Qomah parasangs
precedent and the Qur'an *inshiqāq al-qamar* precedent.

### Ex 13:21-22 — pillar of cloud and fire

- **Canon engagement:** Read as 'Elohim-craft visual marker / spacecraft
  navigation system' in *Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their Planet*.
- **WoH-Translation response:** Surface English preserves the ASV/KJV-baseline
  'pillar of cloud / pillar of fire' theophanic rendering. The central entry
  `pillar-of-cloud-and-fire-ammud-ha-anan-ve-esh-divine-presence-marker-cross-corpus`
  documents (a) the Sommer 2009 fluidity-of-divine-bodies / kavod-YHWH
  visible-presence theology consensus; (b) the cross-corpus reach (Ex 14, Ex 33,
  Ex 40, Num 9, Deut 31, Neh 9, Ps 78, 105, Isa 4:5, 1 Cor 10:1-2, Heb 12); (c)
  operational disavowals against (i) Raëlian-canon Elohim-craft reading
  (acknowledgment-without-endorsement), (ii) Sitchin Anunnaki-aerial-vehicle
  reading, (iii) Christian-typology pillar-as-preincarnate-Christ reading,
  (iv) naturalistic-reduction to volcanic-plume or incense-brazier.

### Ex 14:16-22 — parting of the sea

- **Canon engagement:** Read as 'engineered hydrodynamic-atmospheric
  demonstration of Elohim technology' in *Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their
  Planet*; the canon's most-explicit Raëlian-canon-citation in Exodus alongside
  the Sinai theophany.
- **WoH-Translation response:** Surface English preserves the ASV/KJV-baseline
  'split it ... waters were a wall' rendering. The central entry
  `keriat-yam-suf-parting-of-the-sea-ex-14-divine-warrior-cross-corpus-with-disavowals`
  documents (a) three preserved reading-strata (mainstream Jewish-Christian
  theological miracle-of-deliverance; naturalistic-academic east-wind-tidal-event
  at the Bitter-Lakes shallow yam-suf [Hoffmeier 2005]; Raëlian-canon
  engineered-hydrodynamic-atmospheric-demonstration); (b) operational disavowals
  against (i) Sitchin / Anunnaki engineered-crossing reading, (ii)
  Christian-typological Red-Sea-as-baptism pre-resolution (1 Cor 10:1-2; Heb
  11:29), (iii) Raëlian-canon ET-propulsion pre-resolution
  (acknowledgment-without-endorsement). Additional central entries
  `yhwh-yilachem-lakhem-divine-warrior-formula-ex-14-13-14-cross-corpus`,
  `yam-suf-sea-of-reeds-vs-red-sea-geographical-textual-cross-corpus`, and
  `hardening-of-pharaoh-heart-quishah-lev-ex-7-14-divine-action-theological-problem-cross-corpus`
  handle the related lexical-philological clusters.

### Ex 16:4 — manna

- **Canon engagement:** Read as 'engineered nutrition / Elohim-tech food-drop'
  in *Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their Planet*.
- **WoH-Translation response:** Surface English preserves the ASV/KJV-baseline
  'bread from the heavens' rendering. The central entry
  `mannah-bread-from-heaven-ex-16-quranic-and-nt-cross-corpus-with-disavowals`
  documents (a) five preserved reading-strata (classical Jewish
  miraculous-provision; naturalistic tamarisk-honeydew [Bodenheimer 1947];
  rabbinic-eschatological *mān ha-ganuz* [b. Hag. 12b; Rev 2:17];
  Christian-typological Jesus-as-bread-of-life [John 6]; Raëlian-canon
  engineered-nutrition); (b) operational disavowals against (i)
  simplistic-naturalistic reduction, (ii) Christian-Eucharistic pre-resolution,
  (iii) Raëlian-canon ET-tech-food-drop pre-resolution
  (acknowledgment-without-endorsement).

### Ex 19:16-19 — Sinai theophany

- **Canon engagement:** Read as the canon's most-explicit 'Elohim-craft descent
  on the mountain' citation in *Extra-Terrestrials Took Me To Their Planet*.
- **WoH-Translation response:** Surface English preserves the ASV-baseline
  'thunders and lightnings, and a heavy cloud upon the mountain, and the sound
  of a shofar exceedingly loud' rendering. The central entry
  `sinai-theophany-ex-19-elohim-craft-descent-disavowals-volcanic-hypothesis-cross-corpus`
  documents (a) four preserved reading-strata (mainstream Jewish-Christian
  theological storm-theophany; form-critical theophanic-thunderstorm formula
  [Cross 1973; Day 2000]; volcanic-Sinai academic-historical hypothesis [Stade,
  Wellhausen, Gressmann, Phythian-Adams, Humphreys 2003]; Raëlian-canon
  Elohim-spacecraft-descent reading); (b) operational disavowals against (i)
  Sitchin / Anunnaki engineered-craft reading, (ii) Christian-supersessionist
  Heb 12 Zion-supersedes-Sinai pre-resolution, (iii) Raëlian-canon ET-craft
  pre-resolution (acknowledgment-without-endorsement); (c) the volcanic-Sinai
  academic-historical hypothesis preserved as a documented academic-historical
  hypothesis without adjudication.
