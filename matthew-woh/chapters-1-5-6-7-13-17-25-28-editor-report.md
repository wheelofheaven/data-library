## Lens-discipline assessment

This pass closes the **fourth canon-source-tension after Shi'ur Qomah
(*parasangs*), Qur'an (*inshiqāq al-qamar* + *ḥūr ʿīn*), and Exodus
(pillar/parting/manna/theophany)**. The acknowledgment-without-endorsement
framework is applied at the three Raëlian-canon-flagged sites:

- **Mt 13 Parable of the Sower** — the Raëlian canon (Raël, *The Role of Christ*
  1979) reads the four soil-types LITERALLY as four planet-types receiving the
  Elohim seeding-of-life. This entry **acknowledges-without-endorsing** the
  Raëlian-canon citation. The surface English-translation reads as a defensible
  scholarly rendering at the ASV/RSV-baseline; the three reading-strata
  (mainstream-Christian-traditional / Jewish-historical-critical 1st-c.
  Galilean-Jewish / Raëlian-canon panspermia) are preserved in the apparatus
  without surface-adjudication.

- **Mt 17 Transfiguration** — the Raëlian canon engages the pericope as
  'Elohim manifest in glory + Elohim messenger-figures (Moses + Elijah) appearing
  in their preserved-biological form'. This entry **acknowledges-without-endorsing**
  the Raëlian-canon engagement. The surface English-translation reads as a
  defensible scholarly rendering at the ASV/RSV-baseline.

- **Mt 28 Great Commission** — the Raëlian canon engages Mt 28:18 'all authority
  in heaven and on earth has been given to me' as an Elohim-grant of
  mission-authority (echoing Dan 7:13-14 Son-of-Man enthronement); the threefold
  *patros / huiou / hagiou pneumatos* formula as the Elohim-Son-Spirit-of-Elohim
  triad. This entry **acknowledges-without-endorsing** the Raëlian-canon
  engagement. The Conybeare 1901 textual-variant hypothesis is documented as
  one scholarly position without adjudication.

Additional **lens-discipline operational disavowals** are applied at:

- **Mt 1:22-23** — preserves the LXX *parthenos* / Hebrew *ʿalmâ* philological
  dispute without adjudication; explicitly disavows (i) Christian-supersessionist
  'Jews mistranslated their own scripture'; (ii) Jewish-Christian-polemical
  'Christians invented the virgin-birth from a mistranslation'; (iii) Mariological
  pre-resolution; (iv) Raëlian-canon 'engineered conception' pre-resolution.

- **Mt 5:17** — explicitly disavows Christian-supersessionist / Marcionite /
  dispensationalist / Raëlian-canon 'Elohim-Torah-as-engineered-protocol'
  pre-resolutions.

- **Mt 13:14-15** — flags the Isa 6:9-10 LXX citation as an
  INTERNAL-Israelite prophet's lament that must NOT be deployed in
  Christian-anti-Judaic supersessionist reading.

- **Mt 25:31-46** — preserves the Gray 1989 three-reading-strata without
  adjudication; explicitly disavows Christian-supersessionist anti-Jewish
  Final-Judgment readings; preserves the *aiōnios* philological-breadth
  without pre-committing to eternal-conscious-torment / annihilationism /
  universalism.

- **Mt 28:19** — preserves the Conybeare 1901 textual-variant hypothesis without
  adjudication; explicitly disavows Sitchin / Anunnaki engineered-Elohim-craft
  + orientalist / colonialist Great-Commission misreadings + Raëlian-canon
  Elohim-Son-Spirit-triad pre-resolution.

The translation reflects this lens-discipline by preserving every contested
passage at the defensible scholarly baseline (ASV/RSV/NRSV/SBLGNT critical text)
on the surface and documenting the multi-stratum reading-history in this
glossary apparatus.
