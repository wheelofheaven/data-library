### 12. BCY-WOH-1.4-vii:32 — Baʿlu's voice from the palace

YHWH*), Ps 18:13-14, Hab 3, Job 37, Jer 25:30, Joel 4:16. Cross 1973

### 13. BCY-WOH-1.4-viii:9 — Baʿlu's challenge to Môtu
